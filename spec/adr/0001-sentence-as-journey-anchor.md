# Sentence is the atomic anchor of a journey

A journey is built around exactly one sentence — not a weakness, not a subvirtue, not a virtue. The sentence is a first-person behavioural statement precise enough to anchor specific exposures, resolutions, and a challenge. This makes the journey's scope concrete and personally actionable rather than broad and thematic.

## Considered Options
- **Weakness as anchor** — rejected: too broad; one weakness maps to many subvirtues and dozens of sentences, making ERC selection unfocused.
- **Subvirtue as anchor** — rejected: still too broad; a subvirtue has multiple sentences with meaningfully different behavioural angles.
- **Sentence as anchor** — chosen: specific enough to have a coherent ERC set; precise enough that the vratarthi knows exactly what they are working on.

# PD-039 Deletion, recovery and administration

> **Map:** Distinguish individual record recovery, account exit, moderation restrictions and permanent suspension.

## Common recovery and retention

Normal developmental records remain while active unless eligible deletion applies. Personal deletion uses one configurable X-day recovery period. The expiry calculated when requested is fixed for that request. PH-016 determines the default. On expiry permanently erase subject to specific legal obligations. Legally required information stays outside normal application access with narrow authorization and erasure at legal expiry. No discretionary extra retention, extra recovery duration or automatic inactive-account deletion. Central catalog hard deletion follows PD-038 eligibility.

## Account exit

The Vratarthi may request deletion at any time, including via restricted management while permanently suspended. The final active Admin must first appoint another active Admin. Pending moderation alone cannot block deletion. Preserve legally required case evidence where applicable.

Recovery restricts normal access and hides Profile, owned Journeys, public items and community contributions. Counterpart direct-message history stays available. Cancel pending requests without restoring them. Suspend accepted active guidance/following during recovery. Timely cancellation restores previously active accepted relationships subject to independent restrictions and counterpart eligibility. It does not resurrect previously terminated relationships. Final deletion ends the suspended relationships permanently.

On expiry remove personal records, Profile, own articles and their discussions, own comments/replies with descendants, Journeys and assessments. Preserve counterpart message history, historical contributions to others' Journeys, independently adopted Official/Foundational Resources and legally required data. Credited contributor name and Account deleted label may remain subject to privacy/legal review.

## Journey and assessment recovery

Archive/unarchive is organizational in any lifecycle state. It does not change access, guidance or lifecycle. Discontinuation preserves meaningful history. Whole Journeys and completed assessments are eligible for common recovery. Routine granular deletion of meaningful undertakings or entries is outside initial scope. Unused accidental drafts can be removed. Ordinary authors can correct their own entries without an enforced semantic detector. Legal erasure remains for review.

Journey deletion hides the record, ends Journey-specific guidance and cancels pending Journey invitations and closure-feedback requests. A recoverable unconcluded Journey still occupies the uniqueness slot for its underlying विधान.

**A recovered unconcluded Journey returns to Solo in every case**, including prior Primary-derived mode. Restore the record, historical modes, relationships and contributions. Terminated guidance and cancelled requests remain ended. The owner may explicitly choose Primary-derived guidance or invite a specific person under PD-008. The independent active Primary relationship and its applicable permissions continue. There is no recovery-specific guidance state.

Assessment deletion leaves associated Journeys intact. The specific response and discovery context already consciously incorporated into a Journey remain under that Journey's access policy. The full source assessment and its independent responses follow common recovery. The source link is unavailable until timely recovery restores the source and link. No full assessment copy remains merely through association. Legal erasure still applies.

## Individual article removal

Author deletion immediately hides the article, entire discussion and reactions. Timely common recovery restores the item, prior publication/audience settings, discussion and reactions except independently moderated material. Expiry permanently deletes the article and discussion/reactions subject to legally required retention. References are unavailable while the target is unavailable. Independent adopted Resources survive. Unpublishing and Moderator removal follow their distinct rules.

## Permanent suspension and Admin continuity

Admin-only permanent full-account suspension precludes normal authenticated access and new messaging. It terminates active guidance and following and cancels pending following/message/guidance requests. Permanent suspension revokes any retained historical Vratamitra access, including read-only access as the guide designated at conclusion. Suspension takes precedence over limited late-feedback permission under PD-036. A suspended Vratamitra cannot use a prior request to access the Journey or respond. Historical contributions and counterpart messages persist. An account-level override removes all account-owned published material, shared Journeys, assessments and history from circulation without changing publication, audience, sharing or lifecycle settings. Its comments/replies in others' discussions and descendant branches are hidden. Other-owned Journey contributions remain under those Journeys' rules. Independent canonical Resources persist.

Admin reversal restores normal access and the previously chosen settings subject to independent moderation/removal. Hidden discussion branches may return subject to separate moderation. Reversal does not recreate terminated relationships or restore revoked historical Vratamitra access, including through a prior closure-feedback request. Restricted exit does not bypass suspension. There is no temporary full-account suspension.

Admin grants/revokes Curator, Moderator and Admin capabilities. Controlled bootstrap provisions the first Admin. Normal actions cannot leave zero active Admins, including self-revocation, deletion or suspension of the last one. Audited out-of-band break-glass recovery is required, with technical controls under DT-020. No standing super-admin or blanket private access.

**Basis:** user-confirmed PH-008 handoff, final article/assessment audit resolutions and explicit Solo recovery clarification. Resolves conceptual DT-012/013/024 with PD-036/038.

# PD-038 Community and stewardship authority

> **Map:** Covers publication, discussion, following, protective actions and central catalog authority.

## Knowledge and catalogs

One Curator capability stewards all four developmental catalogs (गुण, उपगुण, उणीवा, गुणदर्शक विधाने), mappings, valid relationships, semantic versions and retirement, ordinary Resources/Sections and their types/tags, and shared अनुभव/संकल्प/आव्हान catalog items. Admins can act as Curators and correct mistakes. No specialist grant or ordinary-user submission flow.

Curators create/edit/publish/withdraw ordinary Resources and may curate/feature. Admin acts or approves when assigning/removing Official or Foundational standing or substantively changing or withdrawing such material. Curators can make meaning-preserving editorial corrections. Public published canonical material is readable unsigned. Drafts remain restricted. Resource references follow current content without a mandatory version system.

Only Admin can permanently delete Resources or Sections. References become unavailable and referencing material survives. No built-in Curator deletion request. For all four developmental catalogs, retire items used in developmental records and preserve historical semantic context and valid dependencies. Admin alone may permanently delete unused items only when no relationship, dependency or historical record breaks. Curators can withdraw shared undertaking items. Admin alone may permanently delete these, preserving existing personal undertakings and making the source reference unavailable.

Curators can feature accessible authored material without transferring ownership or republishing it. Authors retain edit, audience and withdrawal rights. Official/Foundational adoption creates an independent Resource with author permission, attribution and links to the original, without automatic syncing. It remains independently stewarded after author exit subject to lawful rights and attribution. Curator awareness of later edits is an experience question.

## Authored publication and discussion

The author creates, drafts, publishes, edits, unpublishes, republishes and sets audience. Publication and audience are independent. Curator and Primary statuses do not grant draft access. Discussion and reactions follow publication and audience. Unpublishing preserves discussion for republishing. Accessible published items can be read by their audience, with comments, nested replies and reactions available to registered readers. Comment counts are visible to discussion readers. Only each reacted-to item's author sees its reaction count and identities.

A contribution's author may edit/remove their own contribution when authorized. Removal includes descendant replies. The article author may remove another discussion branch with descendants, without editing its words. No generic discussion/reactions on Journeys, undertakings, assessments or Resources/Sections. Individual article deletion is governed by [PD-039](./lifecycle.mdx).

## Following and blocking

Following is directional and recipient-approved. Only the relationship owner sees their follower/following counts and lists, with acceptance dates under PD-027. Request, accept, decline, withdraw, unfollow and remove-follower behavior remains. Following decline is silent and a new follow request is possible absent a block. Detailed request interactions remain DT-030. Blocking ends active/pending following and guidance between parties, cancels pending requests/feedback, revokes special historical guide access, and prevents further messages and mutual authenticated profile/content access. Historical messages and developmental contributions persist. Unblocking does not restore relationships or revoked historical access. Independently public unsigned browsing can remain possible.

## Moderation

Report-accessible material includes inappropriate authored items/comments, received messages and abusive private Journey contributions reported by the Vratarthi whose Journey contains them. Moderators review the reported material and necessary case context only. They can dismiss, warn, remove inappropriate material and impose finite community participation or messaging restrictions with reason and automatic expiry. The affected party gets a high-level explanation and the reporter gets a broad outcome without reporter identity disclosure. Ordinary messaging restrictions can leave formal Journey contributions available. One appeal is reviewed by another Moderator or Admin, with appropriate restoration possible.

Moderation addresses conduct and content. No automatic punishment, philosophical policing or blanket access. Permanent full-account suspension and administrative account deletion are Admin-only.

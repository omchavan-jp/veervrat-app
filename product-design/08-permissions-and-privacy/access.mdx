# PD-036 Authority, audiences and guidance

> **Map:** Identify the permission basis, its purpose and any applicable restriction before disclosing information or allowing action.

## Permission bases

Ownership, authorship and authority are distinct. A Vratarthi controls their practice and cannot rewrite another author's words as their own. Authorship alone confers neither ownership of the containing record nor permanent access. Apply independent bases from ownership, audience, accepted following, guidance or explicitly granted capabilities. Losing one basis leaves unrelated valid bases intact unless a higher-order restriction applies. Curator, Moderator and Admin capabilities alone confer no private Journey, assessment, draft or conversation access.

Protect information across profiles, references, search, notifications, previews and shared records. Deliberately disclosed selections have their own audience. A reference grants no underlying access. No popularity surveillance or engagement pressure.

## Public access and audiences

Public published material, canonical catalogs and Resources, and basic Profiles may be read unsigned, without a public browsing age gate. Registration requires self-declared DOB of at least 18. Under-18 applicants cannot register. Documentary verification is outside the initial scope subject to qualified legal review. Necessity and duration of DOB retention remain legally contingent. Protected account data is not disclosed through Profiles, guidance or capability grants. DT-014 retains additional Profile fields and associated exposure/editing questions.

Journeys, authored items and completed assessments each have a separate adjustable creation default, initially Private, and individual Private, Followers or Public overrides. Defaults apply to new items only. Followers means accepted followers of the owner. Public is available unsigned when publication and applicable restrictions permit. No global account audience or link-only audience. Explicit-person sharing is an uncommitted future candidate.

## Practice control and disclosure

Only the Vratarthi initiates and controls their Journey, chooses guidance, defines/revises/concludes undertakings, pauses/resumes/completes/discontinues practice, chooses audience and history settings, performs assessments, writes final self-review and archives/deletes eligible records. Guides suggest and contribute. Preserve one unconcluded Journey per owner and underlying गुणदर्शक विधान, existing lifecycle rules, and conclusion prerequisites.

Ordinary shared Journey information includes objective, discovery context and relevant विधान-level responses, dates, structure, undertakings and outcomes/lifecycle, and whether there is an effective Journey guide and their identity. It excludes entire assessments, detailed personal accounts/revisions, full history, unrelated private material and observed Active/Inactive engagement. Journey history sharing starts Off. On shares developmental entries, reflections, retrospective entries, guide contributions and past guide identities within the chosen audience. Audience and history changes apply to existing and future history. Independent guidance access remains intact. There are no entry-level audiences.

Acceptance informs guides that present and future sharing includes their contributions and identity, with accurate authorship and no independent veto or identity control. This applies to Primary-derived guidance too. Contributors must understand that present and future active Primaries can see their Journey contributions. Sharing does not automatically include private messages, private invitation history/notes, relationship management notes or unrelated records. Applicable legal rights remain for qualified review.

Registration or invitation alone is not acceptance. Preserve PD-007/008/009 invitation, replacement, overlap, Solo and terminal semantics. Restrictions apply to positive bases.

A valid request made during active Journey guidance can be answered after that relationship ends, before or after conclusion, unless withdrawn by the Vratarthi, cancelled or overridden by restrictions. Provide only the context needed for the response and preserve actual submission timing. Ordinary guidance does not reopen. An earlier guide's request does not substitute for the request to the effective guide at completion. PD-018 prerequisites remain, and DT-023 retains exact waiting, eligibility/expiry and interaction timing.

Authors may edit their own ordinary prose entries only with applicable access. For a meaningful change of understanding, recommend a new entry. No automatic detector or enforcement. The Vratarthi can reflect retrospectively after conclusion, while guide ordinary contribution ends at conclusion. No standalone journal or per-entry privacy controls.

## Assessments

Only the Vratarthi performs assessments. In-progress responses can change and are excluded from community sharing. Completed responses are immutable. Repeat the assessment or delete the old record under the applicable policy. A shared assessment can reference an independently accessible Journey without revealing an inaccessible Journey's existence or details. No assessment discussion, reactions or ranking.

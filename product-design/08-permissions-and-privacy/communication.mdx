# PD-037 Private communication and requests

> **Map:** One conversation per pair, with message requests for unrelated registered Vratarthis and restrictions that can override eligibility.

This user-confirmed PH-008 decision supersedes PD-029 (historical) because the former relationship-only initiation rule excluded requests between unrelated Vratarthis. Preserve pair continuity, reference privacy and the distinction between communication and practice history.

Any unrelated registered Vratarthi can send a private message request, including multiple pending messages without read receipts. Acceptance or reply establishes the conversation. Decline is silent and the sender cannot retry until the recipient reopens. Approved following in either direction or active guidance independently permits normal messaging. An accepted request or reciprocal exchange establishes a conversation that persists after following/guidance ends. Merely sending one-sided messages through a former following relationship does not establish continuing permission.

Use one conversation per pair, even across several Journeys. References grant no access. Mute affects notifications only. Messages do not automatically become entries, evidence or formal closure feedback. Preserve historical messages subject to the recipient and restriction policies in [PD-038](./stewardship.mdx) and [PD-039](./lifecycle.mdx). No End Conversation or My Activity system is approved. Individual sent-message editing/deletion remains outside approved initial operations. Detailed experience is DT-030.

# अनुभव, संकल्प, and आव्हान undertakings

> **Map:** All three types share a flexible lifecycle but retain different purposes and fulfilment meanings. Personal undertakings are distinct from reusable suggestions. Lifecycle, conclusion reason, and outcome are separate; an unsuccessful genuine attempt is not abandonment.

## PD-017 — Undertaking lifecycle, definition, revision, and conclusion

### Definition and fulfilment

| Type | Meaning and essential conceptual parameters |
| --- | --- |
| अनुभव — Exposure | Deliberately undertaken, sufficiently concrete experience to explore/develop the quality, usually bounded to once or a few times. Define intended experience, scope, and completion condition. Completion means genuinely undergoing it; unfamiliarity, challenge, positive learning, and external achievement are not required. Fixed date optional. |
| संकल्प — Resolution | Sustained/recurring personal commitment to cultivate the quality. Define meaningful commitment, qualitative/quantitative practice pattern, sufficiently defined endpoint **before undertaking it**, and fulfilment criteria. No tangible final product required. |
| आव्हान — Challenge | Sufficiently demanding application of developing qualities toward a significant tangible result recognizable by at least one other person, not merely a numerically larger संकल्प. Define undertaking, scope, intended tangible result, and achievement criteria. Successful fulfilment means meeting those defined achievement criteria. Fixed deadline optional. Public recognition, social service, external validation, and Vratamitra approval are not required. Not a mandatory final stage/capstone. Genuine partial or unsuccessful attempts may still be honestly concluded and preserve their developmental significance without being represented as successful fulfilment. |

**Prose-first definitions for all three are confirmed for the initial version.** Essential parameters must be meaningful but need not each be separate structured fields. This consciously favors flexibility, usability, and agency; it does not reject structured identity, type, relationships, lifecycle, or history. More structured definitions/tracking are **uncommitted future candidates**, not deferred implementation obligations.

For संकल्प, intermediate logging and periodic check-ins are optional, with no universal app check-in frequency. A missed day/interruption does not automatically invalidate it. The Vratarthi may reasonably adapt/extend the commitment with revision and reason preserved, or finish at the original endpoint while honestly accounting for interruptions. Revised fulfilment must not be represented as fulfilling the unchanged original. The Vratarthi consciously evaluates fulfilment and records an honest conclusion account; the app must not infer success/failure mechanically from logs, streaks, or thresholds. A genuinely practised 30-day commitment can validly conclude without intermediate logging. **संकल्पपूर्ती is not equivalent to गुणसंवर्धन.**

### Shared lifecycle and flexible participation

All types support **Planned → In Progress → Concluded**, with **Individually Paused / Suspended** and resumption. They may be planned before starting, independently paused, revised, and concluded; multiple undertakings may coexist/overlap in any order within a Journey. Parent Journey pause propagation follows PD-016 and preserves prior individual pause conditions.

Keep three dimensions separate: lifecycle state (where it is), conclusion reason (why it ended), and outcome (what happened/was achieved). Do not fix a detailed outcome taxonomy or persistence structure here. A genuine unsuccessful attempt is not equivalent to abandonment.

Vratamitra planning, refinement, monitoring, and review of individual undertakings is optional. There is **no mandatory Vratamitra approval, validation, or permission to start or conclude** an undertaking. This is distinct from requesting the effective Journey Vratamitra's perspective for parent completion (PD-018).

### Suggestions, revisions, and replacements

Shared catalog suggestions and Journey-specific undertakings remain distinct under PD-014, including many-to-many suggestion–विधान associations. Personal undertakings may originate from shared suggestions or be custom-defined by the Vratarthi/Journey Vratamitra; final authority remains with the Vratarthi. Later shared-suggestion changes must not silently change existing personal undertakings. Custom undertakings are not automatically promoted; curation/promotion remains DT-004 / PH-005.

Meaningful revisions preserve original content, changed content, and reason, and apply prospectively rather than retroactively. The Vratarthi judges revision versus a genuinely new undertaking by fundamental purpose/nature, not rigid property-based rules. Revisions, replacements, withdrawals, and outcomes remain traceable. Exact event/history modeling is DT-025 / PH-007; deletion semantics are DT-024. Detailed contribution permissions remain DT-002.

### Honest conclusion accounts

Undertakings that conclude require an honest account of what happened and its outcome, subject to the specifically approved parent-conclusion exception below; neither a positive result nor a learning claim is required.

When a Journey concludes, already-concluded undertakings remain unchanged; started but unfinished undertakings are accounted for and concluded without false completion. A planned undertaking never started and closed **solely because its parent Journey concludes** may be recorded as **Not Started — Journey Concluded**, without a separate written account. This is an exception/reason, not an additional lifecycle state. Preserve all meaningful history.

That exception does **not** exempt independently withdrawn/cancelled never-started undertakings. Detailed independent-cancellation handling remains for PH-011 under DT-021; no broader account exception is approved here.

DT-021 (PH-009/011) owns controls, presentation, and detailed participation/reiteration/refinement flows; DT-025 owns their history representation. These downstream questions do not reopen the conceptual lifecycle or make additional tracking mandatory.

# Journey lifecycle and conclusion

> **Map:** A Journey begins by conscious choice of one shared विधान. One unconcluded Journey per Vratarthi–विधान is allowed. Lifecycle, observed engagement, and guidance relationship status are separate. Completion and discontinuation require honest self-review but differ in prerequisites.

## PD-016 — Journey initiation, discovery, lifecycle, and engagement

### Identity and discovery

A Journey belongs to one Vratarthi and is anchored to exactly one shared गुणदर्शक विधान (PD-014). It begins when the Vratarthi consciously chooses to work on that विधान; no उणीव, स्वमूल्यांकन, or pre-existing संकल्प is required.

There is **at most one unconcluded Journey per Vratarthi for a particular विधान**: concurrent duplicates are not allowed. Deliberately Paused Journeys and Journeys with Inactive engagement still count. A distinct new Journey after completion is permitted under PD-014; no additional repetition restriction is introduced here.

Discovery may be direct or through one or more उणीवा. Preserve original discovery context separately from subsequent consciously chosen associations, including chronology. When another assessment surfaces the same विधान, indicate the existing Journey; **merely encountering the विधान does not associate the new उणीव**. The Vratarthi may consciously associate it with that Journey. These are historical Journey-specific contexts, not universal catalog mappings. An added context does not automatically change the विधान, undertakings, lifecycle, or completion conditions. Indicators, prompts, and association interactions remain DT-021 (PH-009/011).

### Lifecycle and deliberate pause

| Lifecycle state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Ongoing | The Journey is being undertaken, independently of its observed engagement condition. |
| Deliberately Paused | The Vratarthi explicitly suspends the Journey and its ongoing undertakings; explicit Vratarthi resumption is required. |
| Completed | Conscious conclusion meeting PD-018's completion prerequisites. |
| Discontinued | Conscious conclusion without claiming completion; PD-018 governs its prerequisites. |

Ongoing and Deliberately Paused are **unconcluded**. Completed and Discontinued are terminal conclusions. Pause/resumption preserves history and prior individual undertaking pause conditions: undertakings suspended solely by the parent pause resume with it; individually paused undertakings remain individually paused; never-started undertakings are not started by resumption. No inactivity accumulates while deliberately paused; timing resumes on resumption without erasing earlier engagement history. Detailed timing/pause interactions remain DT-022.

### Observed engagement while Ongoing

**Active / Inactive** is a separate system-observed engagement condition, not lifecycle or guidance status. Inactivity is inferred from an absence of recently recorded meaningful Vratarthi activity. It is not proof that real-world practice, commitment, or गुणसंवर्धन stopped, and does not automatically suspend undertakings or end the Journey.

Qualifying activity is a purposeful recorded Vratarthi contribution toward the Journey's development/practice—for example, planning an undertaking, recording progress, making a meaningful revision, or recording a reflection. These examples are not an exhaustive event taxonomy or approval of a particular recording feature. Inactivity clears automatically on qualifying activity; **opening/viewing/navigation and Vratamitra activity alone do not reset it**. Genuine संकल्प practice without intermediate app records remains valid.

DT-022 (PH-011) owns threshold duration/scope, configurability and authority, exact events, and detailed inactivity/pause interactions. No threshold or compulsory check-in schedule is established. In PD-007/008, **active relationship** means the guidance relationship's status; it must not be interpreted as this Journey engagement condition.

## PD-018 — Journey conclusion, self-review, and terminal guidance

### Logical prerequisites, not a fixed interaction sequence

| Requirement | Completed | Discontinued |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conscious Vratarthi conclusion | Required; meeting a minimum never auto-completes the Journey. | Required; does not claim completion. |
| At least one completed संकल्प | Required; अनुभव and आव्हान remain optional under PD-014. | Not required. |
| Unfinished undertakings | Accounted for and concluded under PD-017 without false completion. | Same honest handling. |
| Final Journey self-review | Mandatory: what happened and how the relevant quality manifested/changed. Uncertainty or no discernible development is acceptable. | Mandatory, with the same honest scope. |
| Effective Journey Vratamitra perspective | Request required if an effective Journey Vratamitra is involved; response/approval not required, no veto. | Request is optional. |

Final Journey self-review is **not formal स्वमूल्यांकन** (PD-013); संकल्पपूर्ती and Journey completion are not themselves गुणसंवर्धन or proof of developmental success (PD-005).

Resolve the effective Journey Vratamitra through PD-008's Primary-derived versus specific-person mode. A different Primary is not an additional required participant; solo Journeys need no Vratamitra feedback. Preserve meaningful request/response history. After a reasonable non-response period, the Vratarthi may proceed. Exact duration and interactions, including self-review versus feedback ordering, remain DT-023 (PH-011); do not prescribe their order here.

### Additive extension of PD-008

For **both Completed and Discontinued** Journeys, active Journey-specific guidance relationships become historical, pending Journey-specific invitations close and cannot later be accepted, and Primary-derived guidance ceases for that Journey. The underlying Primary relationship is unaffected. Preserve prior relationships and contributions. Post-conclusion visibility/access remains DT-002 (PH-008/011).

This explicitly extends PD-008's confirmed completion behavior to discontinuation and states the Primary-derived terminal boundary; existing guidance modes, acceptance, coexistence, replacement, and unilateral ending semantics are unchanged. No supersession is required because the prior rules are retained and the new terminal case is additive.

### Discontinuation, archiving, and deletion

Discontinuation is a developmental/lifecycle conclusion preserving history; archiving is an independent organizational/visibility concern; deletion is a data-management concern, not a substitute for discontinuation. Archive/delete are not Journey lifecycle states, and deletion is not assumed universally prohibited. Eligible states, restoration, retention, cascading effects, and permissions remain DT-024 (PH-008, reconciled PH-009/011).

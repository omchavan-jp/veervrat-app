# Assessment recommendations and continuous development

> **Map:** Assessment is optional discovery, not a Journey prerequisite or proof of success. The initial scale/recommendation model is deliberately simple. Current scope supports continuous development through individual Journeys, without aggregate progression gates.

## PD-019 — Initial assessment and recommendation model

Preserve PD-013's repeatable, point-in-time self-understanding. Two paths are supported:

1. **Assessment:** choose उणीव → associated उपगुण → assess all their गुणदर्शक विधाने → recommendations → consciously choose a विधान → begin or reuse its Journey under PD-016.
2. **Direct:** browse गुण → उपगुण → विधाने → choose a विधान → begin or reuse its Journey without assessment.

The initial version uses **one universal four-level frequency scale: Never → Sometimes → Often → Always** for every विधान. Consciously accepted imperfection: frequency can be less natural for abilities/attitudes than recurring behaviors. Different scales by विधान type or a different universal scale are **uncommitted future candidates**.

Recommendations prioritize comparatively lower-rated विधाने in order **Never, Sometimes, Often, Always**, including when every response is Often or Always. The Vratarthi may choose **any** assessed विधान, not merely recommended ones. No sophisticated scoring/ranking/history-based engine is introduced. Exact count, ties, presentation, and interactions remain DT-026 (PH-009/011).

A future algorithm using historical assessments, ongoing/completed Journeys, developmental progress, or other context is an **uncommitted candidate**, not current scope or an implementation commitment. No numerical scoring formula is implied by the initial ordinal ordering.

## PD-020 — Continuous development without aggregate progression gates

Current application/process scope is **continuous development through individual Journeys**. It does not introduce mandatory aggregate Journey counts, aggregate अनुभव/संकल्प/आव्हान targets, progression levels, formal eligibility/readiness thresholds, or a distinct mandatory milestone for formally undertaking वीरव्रत.

A separate formal undertaking/readiness milestone and possible criteria remain **uncommitted future candidates**, not open obligations to design now. This is a current application/process scope decision, not a philosophical assertion that such a milestone has no place in the wider tradition. Preserve the distinction between Journey completion, genuine developmental success (PD-005), and Veervrat as a संस्कार (PD-003/012).

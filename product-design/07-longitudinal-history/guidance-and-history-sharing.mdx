# Guidance dialogue and history sharing

> **Map:** Vratarthi and relevant Vratamitra contribute contextual developmental dialogue using the same entry concept. Guidance access does not require a privacy choice for each entry. Community history sharing is one optional Journey-level control inside the Journey's existing audience; disclosure safeguards remain PH-008.

## PD-032 Contextual guidance dialogue and Journey history sharing

### Contributors and replies

The Vratarthi and **relevant Vratamitra** can contribute entries to the Journey and its undertakings, using PD-031's generic concept with clear authorship. Distinguish the Vratarthi's own experience/self-understanding from the guide's observations, questions, feedback and perspectives. A guide contributes to the person's developmental history, **not a secret record about them**.

They can reply to one another's entries using the **same generic entry concept with a reference to the earlier entry**. Replies appear in chronological history, without a separate nested community-comment tree. This is the explicitly justified practice-record interaction left open by PD-026; it introduces no separate Journey chat, public discussion system or reactions on developmental entries. PD-029's one private conversation per pair and its separation from Journey history remain unchanged; messages/content/comments do not automatically become entries.

### Guidance boundary without per-entry settings

Vratarthi and relevant Vratamitra access developmental history within the Journey's applicable guidance context. **No individual Personal/Guidance privacy setting** is required for each Journey entry, undertaking entry, reply or guide observation: choosing someone to guide a Journey should not require a new privacy decision for every record.

This is not unrestricted access to other Journeys, assessments or information. Preserve PD-007/008/009's Primary-derived/specific-person modes; do not infer identical Primary rights in every Journey. Exact access/contribution authority, relationship changes, former-guide and post-conclusion access remain DT-002 / PH-008, coordinated DT-029. PD-033 grants only a limited late requested closure response, not restored general guidance.

### Community history-sharing refinement to PD-028

Preserve the **Private / Followers / Public Journey audience**, separate category defaults and individual Journey/authored-content overrides. Add **one optional Journey-level history-sharing control**: the Vratarthi chooses whether developmental history is included in that Journey's selected community audience. No independent undertaking/entry/reply/guide-observation audience controls, approved initial sharing default or new category-default setting are inferred.

| Journey audience | Share history? | Developmental-history audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private | Not applicable to community | Vratarthi and applicable guidance context. |
| Followers | No | Vratarthi and applicable guidance context. |
| Followers | Yes | Above plus approved followers. |
| Public | No | Vratarthi and applicable guidance context. |
| Public | Yes | Above plus applicable Public audience; exact Public Visitor access remains PH-008. |

**Rationale:** a person may share the qualities/undertakings they pursue without exposing personal realizations, difficulties and guidance exchanges; another may share that developmental history more broadly. One audience plus one history-sharing control provides useful flexibility without multiplying privacy settings for each record.

Sharing history **does not widen the Journey's audience or grant community contribution rights**. Community-private does not inherently exclude applicable guidance. Exact boundaries between ordinarily shared Journey/undertaking information and history governed by this control remain PH-008. This is an explicit compatible addition to PD-028, not supersession or a permission matrix.

### Protection against unintended disclosure

A Vratamitra should understand the applicable audience of contributions. Do not silently expose previously private developmental dialogue because a Journey setting changes. **DT-029 / PH-008**, coordinated DT-002/024, must decide changes to history-sharing/audience, effects on existing entries/guide contributions, contributor audience awareness, disclosure protection, any needed consent/authorization, historical/former-guide access and interactions with following, blocking, references, account exit and deletion/retention. Do not choose consent workflows, historical-entry exceptions or per-entry controls now. Experiences remain DT-021/030 / PH-009/011.

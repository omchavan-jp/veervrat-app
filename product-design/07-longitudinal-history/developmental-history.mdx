# Developmental history and entries

> **Map:** History helps the Vratarthi understand development, learn from experience and sustain meaningful guidance. Practice already recorded is not entered again. One prose-first entry concept belongs to a Journey or one of its undertakings; references, chronology and later reflection preserve context without changing past conclusions.

## PD-031 Meaningful history and generic developmental entries

### Purpose and two sources

Developmental history supports **understanding one's development over time** (initial difficulties, undertakings, changed thinking/behaviour/judgment and unresolved matters), **learning from one's experience** (what helped, what did not, why commitments changed and what to carry forward), and **continuity of meaningful Vratamitra guidance** rather than repeatedly starting from scratch.

It helps a person understand development; it is **not proof to the app**. A संकल्प can be genuinely practised without intermediate entries; extensive records or completed undertakings do not automatically establish गुणसंवर्धन. Preserve PD-001/004/005/006/012: no recording compliance, activity scores, streaks, automatic developmental judgments or evidence-volume targets.

| History source | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Naturally arising from practice | Completed assessments; Journey discovery/initiation; undertaking creation/initiation and relevant lifecycle changes; meaningful revisions/replacements/withdrawals; original/changed commitments and reasons; outcomes/conclusion accounts; Journey conclusion/final self-review; relevant guidance relationships, contributions, perspective requests and responses. |
| Consciously recorded | Observations, difficulties, experiences, realizations, progress, failures, insights and reflections that matter to development. No need to create an अनुभव/संकल्प/आव्हान merely to record an observation. |

A person should not separately record what they have already meaningfully done in Veervrat. These sources form coherent chronological history, not every technical event, UI interaction or internal log. Personally written entries appear alongside meaningful practice changes, revisions and conclusions: the **Redmine-ticket-history analogy is a conceptual experience direction**, not a screen layout or event/storage architecture. Presentation/navigation/filtering/controls remain DT-021 / PH-009/010/011. PD-016/017/018's history requirements remain; no mandatory intermediate or daily check-in.

### One entry, two belonging contexts

Use **one generic prose-first developmental entry**, possibly combining observation, experience, difficulty, realization, reflection and progress. No separate Reflection/Observation/Progress Update/Journal Entry/Evidence entities based on purpose, and no mandatory type/category/classification system.

PD-025's authored content supports expression, sharing and community engagement **even when privately published**; developmental entries support the person's practice, understanding and longitudinal history. Purpose, not visibility alone, distinguishes them. Community content, comments and private messages do not automatically become developmental entries.

An entry belongs either to a **Journey generally** or to a **particular personal अनुभव/संकल्प/आव्हान**. An undertaking entry contributes to its own undertaking's history and its parent Journey's overall history as **one entry, not duplicated records**. PD-015 preserves Vratarthi ownership, not Profile ownership. Contributions/replies follow [PD-032](./guidance-and-history-sharing.mdx).

There are **no independent entries outside all Journeys initially**. Pre-Journey discoveries can inform initiation/discovery context; cross-Journey realizations can be recorded in the most relevant Journey with references; later observations can be added to concluded Journeys. This avoids a parallel independent journaling system. Independent entries are an **uncommitted future candidate**, not a later delivery obligation.

### References versus belonging

Entries may reference relevant Journeys/undertakings (including elsewhere), other entries, assessments/relevant responses, गुण/उपगुण/उणीवा/विधाने, Resources/Sections/Handbook/Pothi, undertaking catalog items, authored content and supporting material. These are illustrative targets, not a separate typed association model for every concept. An entry belonging to संकल्प A can reference संकल्प B **without belonging to B's history**. References grant no access; PH-008/011 owns privacy, unavailable-reference and interaction details (DT-002/029/021, Resource policy DT-028).

### Retrospective entries, edits and dates

The **Vratarthi may add retrospective entries** to completed/discontinued Journeys and concluded undertakings. This supports later realizations, even months later, without reopening, changing original conclusion dates/outcomes, rewriting original conclusion accounts/final self-reviews, or restoring former guidance/general contribution permissions. Limited requested late feedback is separately defined in PD-033; no blanket former-guide contribution right is inferred.

Authors may edit their **own ordinary entries**, preserving original creation timing and indicating edits. Minor corrections, clarification and omissions may be edits; a meaningful later change of understanding is preferably a **new entry referencing the earlier one**, preserving evolution. This is guidance, not elaborate enforcement. No comprehensive ordinary-entry edit-version/comparison system initially; this simplification does not weaken PD-017's original/changed commitment and reason requirements. Later reflections cannot rewrite original conclusion accounts or self-review meaning; exact corrections/deletion/retention/permissions remain DT-024/002/029 and experience DT-021.

Automatically preserve **creation date/time and last-edited date/time where applicable**. Entries appear chronologically by creation date; occurrence timing can be described in prose. No separate experience-date/date-range field initially: many observations concern patterns or ongoing realizations without one precise date. Optional occurrence dates/ranges are an **uncommitted future candidate** if useful tracking/analysis needs emerge. Actual lifecycle dates and historical assessment/discovery timing remain unchanged.

## Explanatory projection

Selected Journey history relationships, not a technical event model. Assessments remain independent records; a reference does not add an entry to the referenced item's history.

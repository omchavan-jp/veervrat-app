# Evidence and Journey conclusion continuity

> **Map:** Evidence is the developmental significance of records and experiences, not a new submission system. Final self-review and a guide's supplied closure perspective retain distinct authorship and historical meaning. Late requested feedback and retrospective reflection preserve concluded Journeys rather than reopening them.

## PD-033 Evidence, final self-review and conclusion continuity

### Evidence and meaningful practice history

Evidence is the **developmental significance of existing experiences, records and supporting material**: undertakings/experiences, outcomes/conclusion accounts, entries, guide observations/feedback, self-reviews and referenced material may contribute. There is no separate Evidence entity, submission system, mandatory proof/external validation or numerical developmental score. A completed undertaking or achievement is not automatically proof of गुणसंवर्धन; interpretation concerns actual development (PD-005). Supporting references do not establish mandatory uploads/attachments/storage; evaluate genuine media/reference needs later.

Preserve PD-017: meaningful revisions retain original/changed commitments and reasons, apply prospectively, and keep replacements/withdrawals/outcomes traceable. Conclusion reason/outcome remain distinct from lifecycle; unsuccessful genuine attempts are not abandonment. Honest conclusion accounts remain required with only the approved **never-started undertaking closed solely because its parent Journey concludes** exception. Do not turn all natural history into handwritten entries or require duplicate recording.

### Two complementary conclusion accounts

The Vratarthi's mandatory conscious **final self-review** before completion or discontinuation is **one prose-first account**, supported by guiding questions, not separately required questionnaire answers. Guidance may concern what was undertaken/learned, manifestation/change of the quality, remaining difficulties/uncertainty and self-understanding. References may point to undertakings, entries, experiences and guide observations without repeating all history or producing a numerical final score. It is a considered whole-Journey account at conclusion, distinct from formal स्वमूल्यांकन and ordinary entries, not a generic Reflection system.

Where supplied, the effective Journey Vratamitra's **closure perspective is one meaningful prose-first account** of observed development, experiences, difficulties, limitations and further possibilities. Preserve both complementary contributions—the self-review and feedback if received—with clear authorship and original historical meaning. Disagreement is legitimate; guide feedback is not approval, validation or veto.

### Preserved PD-018 conclusion prerequisites

- Completion requires conscious conclusion, at least one completed संकल्प, appropriate handling of unfinished undertakings, and final self-review.
- Request the **effective Journey** guide's perspective when applicable under PD-008's modes; no extra unrelated Primary response. A response/approval is not required. After a reasonable non-response period, the Vratarthi can consciously complete if other prerequisites hold; waiting expiry neither completes automatically nor creates a partial/pending-approval Journey.
- Solo Journeys need no guide request. Discontinuation requires honest self-review; requesting perspective is optional and completion's minimum संकल्प does not apply.

Exact waiting duration, response interactions and self-review/feedback sequencing remain **DT-023 / PH-011**. No new mechanical completion gate is introduced.

### Limited late requested closure feedback

If feedback was requested **while the guidance relationship was active**, that requested Vratamitra may respond **after Journey completion**. Preserve the meaningful original request, eventual response and actual recording date in conclusion history. This limited response does not reopen the Journey, change completion date, rewrite original self-review, or restore active guidance/general contribution permissions.

Exact eligibility, request/response expiry and access needed to respond remain **DT-002/023 / PH-008/011**; no indefinite access or blanket former-guide permission. A closure-feedback request is not a pending invitation to become Vratamitra: PD-008/018 terminal invitation closure and guidance semantics remain unchanged. PD-031 separately permits the Vratarthi's retrospective entries on completed/discontinued Journeys and concluded undertakings; it grants no unrestricted former-guide participation.

### Continuity rather than general reopening

No general Journey reopening initially. Completion concludes a period of structured practice; it certifies neither perfected quality nor developmental success. Original self-review remains meaningful as understanding at that time. Later realizations should preserve it rather than undo it.

The Vratarthi can record a retrospective entry and consciously initiate a distinct new Journey for the same विधान for further structured practice (PD-016, PD-035). Support continuity through relevant prior context/references so practice need not feel like starting over. Exact carry-forward experience remains DT-021 / PH-009/011: no automatic undertaking copy, guide restart, new-Journey creation or formal continuation workflow. Accidental closure is a later edge-case question, not an approved general reopen capability. Correction/deletion/retention policy remains DT-024.

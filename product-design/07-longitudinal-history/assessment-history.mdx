# Assessment history and discovery context

> **Map:** Completed assessments remain independent point-in-time records; no assessment owns or controls a Journey. Original and consciously added discovery context retain chronology and relevant responses. New practice can start from old history while using the current catalog meaning.

## PD-034 Historical assessments and conscious discovery

### Independent point-in-time assessments

Preserve PD-013/019: स्वमूल्यांकन is optional/repeatable, may assess an उणीव without a Journey, and an existing Journey does not prevent reassessment. Responses concern individual विधाने; **Never → Sometimes → Often → Always** and the ordinal lower-rated recommendation model remain unchanged.

Each **completed assessment remains an independent historical record with all relevant responses**; a new one does not overwrite it. Often → Sometimes is not automatic regression: deeper understanding or more critical self-assessment may alter a response. No automatic progress/regression judgment, historical developmental score or recommendation engine beyond PD-019.

### Discovery does not own or control practice

A Journey belongs to its Vratarthi and is anchored to a विधान, **not owned by an assessment**. It may start from a previous/recent assessment or direct catalog exploration. Other meaningful discovery sources such as Resources or guide suggestions can be preserved as context without an elaborate source taxonomy. No compulsory latest assessment, assessment expiry, reassessment prerequisite or need to reconcile differing ratings first.

**Illustration:** June assessment of उणीव 1 includes GV1/GV2, followed by a GV1 Journey. October reassessment may differ. In November the Vratarthi may choose GV2 from June, October or direct discovery—even if GV2 was rated Always in October. Both assessments remain intact; neither controls that choice.

### Original and additional contexts

Preserve original discovery context, additional **consciously associated** contexts, relevant chronology (assessment timing and association timing) and relevant historical responses. The full assessment stays independent: reference it rather than requiring its data to be duplicated into the Journey.

| Illustrative context for GV2 | Preserved historical response |
| --- | --- |
| Original June assessment of उणीव 1 | Sometimes. |
| Consciously added October assessment of उणीव 1 | Always. |
| Consciously added December assessment of उणीव 2 | Often. |

The original remains unchanged. Merely encountering the विधान in later assessment does **not** automatically associate that assessment/उणीव with the Journey. These are Journey-specific contexts, not a direct catalog उणीव–विधान relation. Preserve PD-016's one-unconcluded-Journey-per-Vratarthi–underlying-विधान restriction, including paused/inactive Journeys and different semantic versions.

### Historical source, current objective

Under [PD-035](./catalog-continuity.mdx), a new Journey initiated from an older assessment uses the **current semantic version** of a non-retired विधान. The old assessment preserves its previous version/response; historical discovery and current objective may legitimately differ. Make meaningful change apparent before conscious initiation, without mandatory reassessment or choosing historical versions. DT-021/026 / PH-009/011 owns historical navigation, existing-Journey indicators and context-association interactions; catalog-change awareness also reconciles DT-027.

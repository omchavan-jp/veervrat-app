# Developmental catalog continuity

> **Map:** Preserve the past, but begin new practice against current developmental catalog meaning. Identity and semantic version differ; relationship context can also change without changing meaning. Ongoing Journeys retain their objective, while retirement preserves history and prevents new practice against retired विधाने.

## PD-035 Historical meaning, semantic versions and catalog continuity

### Meaning-preserving corrections versus semantic change

Apply historical preservation to all four shared PD-013 catalogs: **गुण, उपगुण, उणीवा and गुणदर्शक विधाने**. Assessments/Journeys retain meaning and relevant context as they existed at the relevant time; maintenance must not silently rewrite what was assessed, undertaken or understood.

- **Minor meaning-preserving correction:** edit the existing version, e.g. spelling, grammar or wording without changed meaning. No semantic version or full previous-phrasing archive for every correction.
- **Meaningful semantic change:** create a new semantic version of the **same underlying entity**, preserving the earlier version for historical context. Conceptual identity and version are distinct.
- A **fundamentally different concept** may warrant a new catalog entity rather than another version. No rigid automatic criteria are selected here.

PD-024's optional description remains part of its विधान; clarification versus meaningful change depends on actual developmental meaning. Preserve relevant meaning/relationships without mandating comprehensive editorial audits, full-text snapshots of minor edits, compare/rollback interfaces or persistence architecture. Semantic preservation is required; elaborate version management is not automatically approved.

### Existing versus new Journeys

A Journey stays anchored to the **semantic version of its विधान at initiation**. Existing ongoing/paused Journeys continue with that objective, undertakings, completion and self-review; minor meaning-preserving corrections may be reflected. No automatic semantic replacement, undertaking migration or change to existing practice.

**Conscious version switching within an ongoing Journey is excluded initially.** Changing meaning during practice complicates undertaking relevance, earlier history and the final self-review's objective. A Vratarthi may consciously conclude existing practice and initiate a new Journey against current meaning, preserving continuity (PD-033). At most one unconcluded Journey per **underlying विधान**, regardless of versions: different versions do not permit concurrent duplicates. Conscious switching with meaningful transition history is an **uncommitted future candidate** if justified later.

**Example:** June assessment records GV2 Version 1; October introduces meaningful Version 2; November new Journey from June history uses current Version 2. June retains Version 1/responses. Make the meaningful change apparent before conscious initiation; no mandatory reassessment or historical-version choice. Preserve past history while starting new practice against current meaning, subject to retirement. Awareness/confirmation experience remains DT-021/026/027 / PH-009/011.

### Retirement is not deletion

For a **retired विधान**, historical assessments/responses and completed/discontinued Journeys remain intact. Existing ongoing/paused Journeys may continue against their original semantic version and conclude normally. **No new Journey** can start for that retired विधान; no automatic discontinuation, replacement or forced migration. Historical assessments remain meaningful when earlier concepts are unavailable for new practice. Preserve Vratarthi authority over existing practice.

Apply underlying historical preservation to गुण/उपगुण/उणीवा appropriately. Exact retirement/deletion authority, maintenance flows, reference/lifecycle handling remain downstream; no hard deletion, permanent retention, restoration policy or automatic successor mapping is inferred.

### Relationship changes and valid active catalogs

PD-013's constraints remain: exactly one गुण per उपगुण; exactly one उपगुण per विधान; उणीव–उपगुण many-to-many; no direct canonical उणीव–विधान link.

| Change | Historical and current boundary |
| --- | --- |
| विधान moves to another उपगुण | Earlier assessments/Journeys preserve relevant original context; new practice uses current classification. |
| उणीव's associated उपगुण change | Earlier assessed statements/responses remain; new assessments use updated associations. |
| गुण or उपगुण retires | History remains; dependent active catalog items must be deliberately reassigned or retired, not left without valid parents. |

A relationship change need not create new semantic versions of every related entity if meaning is unchanged. Preserve relevant historical relationship context separately where necessary, without selecting technical representation. Neither Resource current-content rules nor undertaking-catalog lifecycle/history rules transfer by analogy; PD-021/022/023 and DT-028 remain intact.

### Comprehensive later stewardship without unnecessary complexity

DT-012 / PH-008 owns create/edit/version/retire/reclassify authority for all four catalogs. DT-027 / PH-009 owns complete maintenance and Vratarthi change-awareness experiences; PH-010 owns organization/destinations/authorized navigation. PH-011 / DT-027 owns flows, dependencies, validation/errors, impact awareness/confirmation, retirement/reclassification and invalid-relationship prevention. PH-012–015 retain terminology/design-system/interaction/screen work; DT-020 / PH-016 retains representation, historical integrity, migration/compatibility, sequencing and design QA.

Later retirement design must consider dependent विधाने, associated उणीवा, impact before confirmation, valid parents and preserved practice/history. This does not require elaborate editorial approval hierarchies. **Being comprehensive and being unnecessarily complicated are not the same thing.**

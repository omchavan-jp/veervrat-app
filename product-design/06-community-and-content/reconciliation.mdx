## Representative scenario reconciliation

| Scenario | Consistency result |
| --- | --- |
| Philosophical article independent of a Journey | Generic authored content supports broader interests without Journey prerequisites or initial tags. |
| Article about a private Journey/संकल्प | Ordinary references support discussion without granting access to the underlying practice. |
| A follows B after B accepts, without reciprocity | Both may initiate private communication; A may belong to B's Followers audience, not automatically the reverse. |
| Same Vratamitra across multiple Journeys | One conversation per pair avoids fragmentation; meaningful developmental history remains a separate PH-007 question. |
| Generally shared Journeys, one sensitive exception | Separate category defaults plus individual overrides preserve convenience and personal control; actual defaults remain undecided. |
| Editing published content through a working draft | Existing published material stays available to its audience while one unpublished draft is prepared; identity is retained. |
| Recipient reports a private message | Relevant reported material can support review without unrestricted Moderator access to the whole conversation. |
| Blocking someone who is also a Vratamitra | Protective purpose is confirmed; consequences for guidance, access, communication and restoration are explicitly deferred. |

These are conceptual consistency checks, not exhaustive user-flow tests or additional features.

## Scope exclusions and uncommitted candidates

Current scope has no separate Post/Blog/Article/Daily Log entities based on length/subject, authored-content tags, direct community comments/reactions on Resources/Sections or Journeys/undertakings, multi-reaction system, popularity ranking, visitor tracking, friendship relationship, unrelated-person message requests, or separate Journey conversations. Group chat, calls, message formats/attachments/read receipts and other messaging features are not established; later design of the confirmed capability does not make every possible feature a requirement.

Uncommitted candidates include authored-content tags potentially reusing Resource tags; elaborate content version history/comparison/restoration/revision management; structured strikes, automated moderation scoring/detection and sophisticated enforcement workflows. These are neither implementation obligations nor permanent philosophical prohibitions. Practice-record interactions require a genuine PH-007 need before approval.

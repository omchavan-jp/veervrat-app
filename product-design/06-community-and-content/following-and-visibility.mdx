# Following and item visibility

> **Map:** Following is an accepted one-way contextual relationship, independent of guidance. Community visibility is chosen per Journey and authored-content item, with separate category defaults for convenience. Audience policy and relationship-change effects remain deferred; no global Public/Private account type is introduced.

## PD-027 Approved following relationship

A Vratarthi may want to remain connected with another's shared content, perspectives and developmental experiences without guidance. **A may follow B without B following A**; mutual following is unnecessary. Follower is a contextual relationship, not a granted platform capability (PD-011). No separate friendship/connection relationship in current scope.

Following requires **explicit recipient acceptance**: A sends B a request; following becomes active only when B accepts. Followers-only denotes an accepted audience, not anyone choosing to follow. Do not introduce public/private account types or automatically open following.

Support sending, accepting/declining, withdrawing an outgoing pending request, unfollowing and removing a follower. Either party can end active following. **No explicit decline notification**; a declined request is no longer pending. Re-request rules, expiry, duplicates, blocked relationships and detailed state interactions remain DT-029/030; no elaborate history of every past follow/unfollow/decline is introduced.

Each Vratarthi can see incoming requests; outgoing pending requests **and when sent**; current followers **and when their requests were accepted**; current following **and when their requests were accepted**. List presentation is later experience work. Visibility of another person's lists remains PH-008. No public follower-count competition or popularity ranking.

### Independent of Vratamitra guidance

Neither party must follow the other to establish Primary or Journey guidance. Becoming Vratamitra creates no automatic following in either direction; ending following does not itself end guidance, and ending guidance does not itself end following. PD-007/008/009's guidance eligibility, lifecycle, invitation history and scope are preserved. The simpler following-history model does not apply to guidance relationships. Blocking is separate; its consequences for both remain PH-008/011 under PD-030 / DT-029.

## PD-028 Item audiences with category defaults

| Audience | Conceptual meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Not shared with the community; established guidance access is a separate consideration. |
| Followers | Shared with the owner's approved followers. If A follows B, A may belong to B's audience; B does not automatically belong to A's. |
| Public | Shared with a broader audience; exact Public Visitor/unsigned-in access remains PH-008. It does not automatically mean everyone on the internet. |

The Vratarthi chooses visibility of **individual Journeys** and **individual authored-content items**. Support one category default for Journeys and a **separate** category default for authored content, with individual overrides. Do not require an entire account to be Public or Private, or force a choice between purely global and purely per-item control.

### Durable rationale and illustrative example

Category defaults reduce repetitive creation-time decisions; individual overrides preserve control over sensitive sharing. A person should not need to change every Journey or their entire account just to protect one personal item.

**Illustration only:** suppose this Vratarthi chooses Followers for their Journey category and Public for their authored-content category:

| Item | Illustrative individual choice |
| --- | --- |
| Journey A — development they wish to share | Followers, using their chosen category default. |
| Journey B — particularly personal difficulty | Private, overriding that default. |
| Authored item A — philosophical article | Public, using their chosen content default. |
| Authored item B — personal difficulties | Followers, overriding that default. |

These are **not approved actual initial default values**. The example preserves convenient defaults and item-level flexibility together.

### Related boundaries and deferred policy

A community-private Journey is not necessarily inaccessible to its established Vratamitra. Guidance access and community audience are distinct. Authored content referencing a private Journey grants no access to it. Changing category defaults and changing one item's audience are distinct conceptual actions. Prefer Journey-level visibility over unnecessary separate controls for each undertaking; exact inheritance/exceptions remain undecided. Discussion follows its authored-content boundary (PD-026). Assessment privacy and Profile information have their own policy; these category defaults do not automatically govern them.

**PH-008 / DT-029** owns exact audiences/Public Visitor access, actual initial defaults, effects of default changes on existing items, inheritance/exceptions across Journeys/undertakings/practice records/comments/references, guidance access, and access consequences of following/guidance/block or audience changes, including existing content/comments/reference visibility. Coordinate DT-002/012/013/014/024/028; no permission matrix or persistence model is decided here. Settings, creation/management controls, placement and detailed flows remain DT-030 / PH-009/010/011.

**Compatible PH-007 refinement to PD-028:** [PD-032](../07-longitudinal-history/guidance-and-history-sharing.mdx) adds one optional Journey-level history-sharing control within the existing Journey audience, without individual entry/undertaking/reply/guide-observation audiences, initial sharing default or new category-default setting. Separate category defaults and item overrides remain. Existing-contribution disclosure protection, contributor awareness and exact access policy stay DT-029/002/024; no consent workflow is selected and PD-028 is not superseded.

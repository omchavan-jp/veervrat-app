# Discussion and appreciation

> **Map:** Discussion belongs to authored content, with nested replies and one simple appreciation capability. Reaction counts belong to each item's own author, not its discussion's host. Curated knowledge and personal practice have no direct generic community comments/reactions in current scope.

## PD-026 Discussion, appreciation, and interaction boundaries

Vratarthi may comment on other Vratarthis' authored content for discussion, questions, perspectives, encouragement and intellectual exchange. A comment may respond to the content; a reply may respond to a comment or reply. **Nested discussion is supported**, without deciding a maximum depth, sorting, layout, collapsing or pagination. Comments/replies follow the authored content's audience/visibility boundary; exact access and lifecycle consequences remain PH-008/011.

Support **one simple reaction capability**, such as Like or Appreciate, on authored content, individual comments and nested replies. It permits meaningful appreciation without a written response. Final terminology/interface remain later; no multiple reaction types or complex emoji system initially.

| Count | Confirmed visibility |
| --- | --- |
| Authored-content reaction count | Only that content's author can see it. |
| Comment reaction count | Only that comment's author can see it. |
| Reply reaction count | Only that reply's author can see it. |
| Comment/reply counts | May be visible to people who can access the discussion, indicating discussion exists rather than approval. |

**Other Vratarthis cannot see these reaction counts.** An article author does not gain reaction-count access to someone else's comment/reply merely by hosting the discussion. Exact presentation and reaction-giver identity visibility remain undecided (DT-029/030).

Appreciation encourages recipients without becoming a public popularity measure. **No popularity ranking, trending-author system or reaction-based gamification.** Do not sort or prominently highlight comments by likes/popularity in ways that restore those dynamics. No unnecessary engagement analytics, post-view counts, Profile/Journey visitor tracking or identification of mere viewers. This does not prohibit “views” meaning screens/destinations/presentation modes; those belong to experience/navigation design. Preserve PD-004/005/006/012.

### Where community interaction occurs

- **Resources/Sections:** no direct community comments or reactions. A Vratarthi may author content referencing a Resource and discuss it there; curated knowledge and community discussion remain distinct.
- **Journeys and personal अनुभव/संकल्प/आव्हान:** no direct generic community comments or reactions in current scope. Authored content about development provides the community discussion context. Making a Journey visible does not require comments/reactions on it.

The Journey boundary reflects deliberate reconsideration: a generic Journey comment section was not sufficiently justified before PH-007 defines practice-record/reflection mechanisms. Interactions on particular future practice records may be reconsidered only if PH-007 identifies a genuine need; they are **not preapproved**.

This does not remove Vratamitra guidance, contributions, feedback or conclusion obligations in PD-007/008/014/016/017/018. Generic community discussion is distinct from those established relationships and obligations. PH-007 / DT-025 owns meaningful developmental records, PH-008 / DT-002/029 owns permissions, and DT-030 owns discussion/reaction experiences.

**Subsequent PH-007 resolution:** [PD-031/032](../07-longitudinal-history/README.mdx) justify contextual developmental dialogue: the same generic entry concept can reply by referencing an earlier entry in chronological history. This is not generic community comments/reactions on Journeys or undertakings, not a nested community tree or separate Journey chat. PD-026's community boundary remains intact; no supersession.

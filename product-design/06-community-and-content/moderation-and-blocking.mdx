# Moderation and personal blocking

> **Map:** Moderation protects meaningful engagement and privacy; it does not judge philosophical correctness or developmental success. Reports prompt review, not automatic punishment. Personal blocking is separate from moderation, with consequential access/relationship policy deliberately deferred.

## PD-030 Moderation, reporting, and personal blocking

### Purpose and role boundary

Moderation protects engagement from harassment, abuse, spam and disruption; maintains appropriate content standards **without requiring every contribution to concern personal development**; and protects Vratarthi while respecting privacy.

Moderators address inappropriate content/conduct, not intellectual disagreement or the quality of someone's development. They do not judge whether a person practises Veervrat correctly, holds the correct philosophical opinion or develops sufficiently. Questioning an idea or disagreeing with a practice is not by itself grounds for moderation.

Curator stewards canonical/curated knowledge; Moderator addresses inappropriate community content/conduct. Preserve PD-011's granted-capability model; exact authority is PH-008 / DT-012/029.

### Reporting, review and possible action

A Vratarthi may report authored content, comments/replies, inappropriate private messages **they receive**, and harassment/other inappropriate conduct by another Vratarthi. A report brings a matter to a Moderator; it establishes neither a violation nor automatic punishment.

Use a simple **reporting → review → appropriate action** model. Conceptual actions are:

- Dismiss when no violation is found or no action is necessary.
- Warn a Vratarthi.
- Remove inappropriate content from circulation.
- Restrict participation for serious/repeated inappropriate behaviour.
- Escalate matters needing broader authority to Admin.

These are capabilities, not sanction levels, permission grants, thresholds, appeal rules or enforcement workflows. Exact authority, visibility contexts, restrictions, review access and escalation conditions remain PH-008/011 (DT-029/030).

Moderators have **no automatic unrestricted access** to private Journeys, practice records or conversations. Reporting a received inappropriate message may make relevant reported material available for review, without exposing the full conversation history. Exact report material, evidence-access mechanism, privacy/review and retention rules remain deferred; no surveillance access is inferred.

Structured strikes, automated moderation scoring/detection and sophisticated enforcement hierarchies/workflows are **uncommitted future candidates**, not approved initial features or promised later deliverables. No automatic punishment, unnecessary surveillance or elaborate scoring.

### Personal blocking and unblocking

A Vratarthi may block/unblock another independently of moderation. Blocking protects against unwanted direct communication/community interaction; it is a **personal protective action**, not a platform punishment, and does not automatically file a report.

PH-008/011 / DT-029/030 must decide consequences for pending/existing following, visibility/access, initiation/continuation of communication, existing conversation/history access, Primary/Journey guidance, and unblocking/restoration. Do not assume that blocking ends guidance, preserves all access, deletes history or restores old relationships on unblock. The protective purpose is confirmed; those consequential policies are not.

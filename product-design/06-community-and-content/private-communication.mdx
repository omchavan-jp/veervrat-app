# Private communication

> **Map:** Private communication supports meaningful community exchange and guidance beyond Vratamitra pairs alone. Approved following in either direction or established guidance permits both people to initiate. One conversation per pair avoids Journey-by-Journey fragmentation; messages are not automatically practice history.

## PD-029 Relationship-based private communication

Veervrat supports private communication between Vratarthis generally, not only between guides and those they guide, without aiming to become a full messaging platform.

| Relationship basis | Eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| Active approved following in either direction | Both participants may initiate; mutual following is not required. If A follows B after B accepts, both A and B may initiate even if B does not follow A. |
| Established Primary or Journey Vratamitra relationship | Independently permits private communication without following. Preserve PD-008's Primary-derived versus specific-person guidance semantics. |
| Unrelated Vratarthis | No private messaging by default; no separate message-request system in current scope. |

Exact restrictions, blocking and sending/access consequences after relationships end remain PH-008/011 (DT-029/030). Eligibility is not a blanket grant to referenced information.

### One conversation per pair

Use **one private conversation between any two Vratarthis**, regardless of how many Journeys/guidance relationships they share. No separate Journey-specific private conversation initially. A Primary Vratamitra may also guide multiple Journeys with the same person; overlapping discussions would otherwise fragment across a general chat and several Journey chats, creating unnecessary management complexity.

Messages may optionally reference Journeys/undertakings, Resources/Sections, विधाने or other relevant Veervrat concepts, subject to applicable access rules. Contextual references identify the discussion's subject without multiplying conversation spaces.

**Private conversation history is not Journey history.** Not every message becomes a developmental record, formal Vratamitra feedback or evidence. PH-007 / DT-025 will address how meaningful guidance, observations and feedback enter developmental history. PD-018's perspective-request and self-review obligations remain intact; an ordinary message is not automatically their fulfilment.

No group chat, calling, message-format/attachment, delivery/read-receipt, editing/deletion, retention or messaging implementation model is established here. Necessary policy/experience details for the confirmed capability remain DT-029/030 and implementation DT-020; possible messaging features are not automatically requirements.

**Subsequent PH-007 resolution:** [PD-031/032/033](../07-longitudinal-history/README.mdx) establish generic developmental entries/contextual replies and prose-first conclusion accounts without converting ordinary private messages into entries, formal feedback or evidence automatically. PD-029's one conversation per pair and separation from Journey history remain unchanged; no separate Journey chat or supersession.

# Community and authored content

> **Map:** Authored content can concern personal development or independent interests. One conceptual model supports short thoughts and detailed articles, without tags initially. Publication state, working edits, audience, and removal are separate; personal practice and curated Resources remain distinct.

## PD-025 Community purpose and authored content

### Purpose and agency

Community serves **mutual learning** from experiences, perspectives, successes and difficulties; **mutual inspiration/encouragement** in development and व्रतपालन; and **meaningful intellectual/social engagement** around ideas, knowledge, personal development or broader interests, independently of a particular Journey.

**Veervrat is not generic social media.** Community engagement, popularity and public sharing are not prerequisites for Veervrat or successful व्रतपालन. Community supports agency (PD-004); visibility, reactions, followers and activity metrics are not developmental success (PD-005). समाजाभिमुखता is not public posting, engagement metrics or visibility (PD-012). Preserve कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन (PD-001) and notification constraints (PD-006). No conventional social feed, engagement optimization, recommendation engine or trending system follows from community's existence.

### One authored-content concept

A Vratarthi may create **authored content** with an **optional title**, a **content body** and an **original author**. Short thoughts and detailed articles with headings/references/examples share this concept. Subjects can arise from development, Journeys, experiences, reflection, difficulties, realizations or accomplishments, or from independent learning, philosophy, history, interests, questions and perspectives. Subject/length alone creates no separate Post, Blog, Article, Journey Post, Experience Post or Daily Log entity.

“Authored content” is conceptual terminology; final product-facing naming remains PH-012. Editor, formats, media/attachments and representation remain undecided. A Journey, विधान or undertaking is not required. Authored content belongs to its author/Vratarthi; a Profile may display it without owning it (PD-011/015).

A personal practice record is distinct from community-authored content. An article, comment or message does not automatically become formal reflection, evidence, self-review, Daily Log or an undertaking conclusion account. PH-007 / DT-025 owns actual practice-history/recording models; PD-014/016/017/018 remain intact.

### References, tags and curated knowledge

Authored content may optionally reference Resources/Sections; गुण, उपगुण, उणीवा and विधाने; अनुभव/संकल्प/आव्हान catalog items; and personal Journeys/undertakings subject to access rules. Ordinary references suffice; no new formal association model is required. **A reference grants no access**: readers of an article about a private Journey do not thereby gain access to that Journey. Reference/access/unavailability details remain PH-008/011 (DT-029/030).

**No authored-content tags initially**: no taxonomy, free-form tags, hashtags, tag requests or approved reuse of Resource tags. Possible future reuse of the shared Resource subject-tag collection is an **uncommitted candidate**. PD-022's Resource/Section types, tags and developmental associations remain unchanged.

Authored material is not automatically a curated Resource, and article-like content does not collapse these concepts. Curators may consider valuable authored material for curated inclusion while preserving original authorship; curation does not confer foundational/official standing (PD-015/021). No dedicated Vratarthi submission/promotion/approval/rejection workflow is required; no private-content access or appropriation permission is granted. Exact relationships/access reconcile under DT-028 and PH-008/011 experience work.

### Publication lifecycle and working edits

Support **Draft → Publish → Edit**, **Unpublish back to draft → Republish**, and **Remove**. These are conceptual capabilities, not a persistence state machine. Removal/deletion/retention consequences remain PH-008/011. No mandatory pre-publication editorial approval for ordinary authored content.

An **unpublished draft** and a **privately published item** differ: draft concerns readiness/publication, Private concerns audience (PD-028). An author may change a published item's audience independently of editing or publication state; Public → Followers/Private does not require returning to draft.

Unpublishing makes the item unavailable to its previous community audience, preserves the same authored-content identity, and permits editing/republishing. Existing comments/reactions are **not automatically erased** by unpublishing; exact access, restoration and retention remain DT-029/030.

A published item may optionally have **one unpublished working draft of edits**. The current published material remains available under its visibility while the author prepares changes privately; publishing those edits updates the same item's published content. This is one published version plus an optional working draft, not comprehensive version management. Do not transfer this capability to PH-005 Resources automatically.

Content version history, comparison/restoration of earlier versions and sophisticated draft/revision management are **uncommitted future candidates**, not deferred implementation obligations. Exact authoring/publication/visibility experiences remain DT-030; policy DT-029; implementation DT-020.

## Explanatory projection

Selected publication transitions only; working edits are not a second authored-content item. Audience/removal policy remains governed by MDX.

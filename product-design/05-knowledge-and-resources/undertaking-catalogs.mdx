# Undertaking catalogs and Resource boundaries

> **Map:** Curators maintain reusable अनुभव, संकल्प, and आव्हान catalog items. Personal undertakings belong to Journeys. Relevant catalog entries are selectable without an undertaking recommendation engine. Resources supply knowledge; catalog items supply ideas for practice.

## PD-023 — Undertaking catalog terminology and stewardship

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Undertaking catalog item | Centrally curated, reusable अनुभव, संकल्प, or आव्हान that a Vratarthi can choose to undertake. Earlier PD-014/017 wording **“shared suggestions” / “catalog suggestions”** maps to this concept. |
| Personal undertaking | Actual Journey-specific अनुभव, संकल्प, or आव्हान, chosen, adapted, or custom-defined. |
| Recommendation | Veervrat specifically recommends one or more items as particularly relevant; merely presenting associated catalog entries is not a recommendation mechanism. |

This explicitly clarifies PD-014/017 terminology, without superseding their ontology, relationships, or decision history. Vratamitra suggestions remain meaningful; PD-019's confirmed **विधान recommendations during self-assessment are unchanged**.

Curators create, maintain, and publish shared undertaking catalog items. Catalog item–गुणदर्शक विधान associations remain **many-to-many**. A Vratarthi may choose relevant items, adapt them, or define custom undertakings; the Journey Vratamitra may contribute custom ideas. **Final authority remains with the Vratarthi.** Personal undertakings remain distinct; catalog edits must not silently alter existing personal undertakings (PD-017).

For example, when selecting an अनुभव for विधान X, making X's associated catalog items available is sufficient in current scope. **No separate undertaking recommendation engine is approved**; selecting a particularly suitable subset remains an uncommitted future candidate.

Personal undertakings do not automatically become catalog items. There is **no dedicated Vratarthi catalog submission or promotion workflow in current scope**. A Curator could independently create an item inspired by a useful idea, subject to privacy/access rules; this grants neither access to private Journeys nor permission to republish personal material. Exact stewardship/contribution permissions remain DT-012/028 and relevant DT-002 work in PH-008.

### Resources are distinct

Resources provide knowledge/understanding; undertaking catalog items provide reusable ideas for practice/application. अनुभव, संकल्प, and आव्हान **are not Resource Types**. A catalog item may optionally reference a Resource or Section in its prose-first description—for example, a संकल्प involving daily reflection on a Pothi श्लोक. There is **no separate formal Resource–Undertaking association model**, Resource-linking subsystem, or mandatory Resource selection. This ordinary supporting-reference capability preserves PD-017's prose-first definitions.

DT-004's conceptual catalog scope is resolved here. DT-027 owns discovery/selection/authoring/reference experiences; DT-017 retains authored-content relationships in PH-006; PH-008 owns access and stewardship policy. No editor, social feature, or implementation model is decided.

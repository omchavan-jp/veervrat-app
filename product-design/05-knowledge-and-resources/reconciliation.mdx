## Conscious simplifications and candidates

Current scope deliberately uses generic Resources/Sections, extensible types/tags, optional associations, simple Curator publishing, current-content references without mandatory Resource history, and relevant undertaking-catalog access without a recommendation engine.

Separate Collections/ordered reading lists, dedicated Vratarthi submission/promotion workflows, mandatory multi-person editorial approval, mandatory Resource history/snapshots, specialized format-specific entities, a separate undertaking recommendation mechanism, and a formal Resource–Undertaking association model are **not current-scope requirements or deferred delivery obligations**. Collections, elaborate Resource history, and undertaking recommendations may be reconsidered only as uncommitted future candidates if a genuine need emerges. This does not weaken personal-history requirements or grant new permissions.

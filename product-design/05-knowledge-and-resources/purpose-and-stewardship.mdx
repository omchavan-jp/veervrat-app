# Knowledge purpose, standing, and stewardship

> **Map:** Knowledge may explain Veervrat, support development, or enable broader learning. Authorship, foundational/official standing, curation, and featuring have different meanings. Curators steward the curated knowledge collection with a simple publishing lifecycle; detailed authority and access remain deferred.

## PD-021 — Knowledge purpose, standing, and stewardship

### Purpose and standing

Preserve PD-015: a **Resource** is material made accessible through Veervrat to support understanding, learning, or personal development. Knowledge and Resources serve:

1. Understanding Veervrat's philosophy, purpose, principles, concepts, and संस्कार.
2. Supporting गुणसंवर्धन, संकल्पपालन, and individual Journeys.
3. Broader learning and intellectual exploration, independent of a current Journey, उणीव, or developmental goal.

Veervrat may be a learning destination in its own right, governed by PD-001's North Star. This neither commits to a generic knowledge platform nor requires knowledge consumption in a Journey (PD-004/005).

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Authorship | Who created the material; preserve original authorship. |
| Foundational standing | Establishes or authoritatively explains Veervrat's philosophy, principles, concepts, or approach. |
| Official standing | Represents Veervrat's own official position or publication. |
| Curation | Deliberate selection for value; not necessarily endorsement of every opinion expressed. |
| Featuring | Deliberately giving material particular prominence. |

These distinctions are not mandatory database fields, independent workflow systems, or mutually exclusive classifications. **Handbook and Pothi currently have foundational standing**, but the foundational body is not fixed at two documents: additions, revisions, replacements, and removal of standing are possible. Exact authority remains PH-008; this grants no arbitrary contributor modification rights.

Supplementary material enriches understanding, offers perspectives, supports development, or enables broader learning without necessarily representing the foundational position. Not all official material is foundational; not all curated material is official.

### Curator stewardship and publishing

Curators manage the centrally curated knowledge collection (PD-011). A Curator may author an article or select valuable material created by someone else for curated inclusion and broad prominence. Preserve original authorship. Curation/featuring does not itself confer foundational or official standing.

The initial publishing lifecycle is deliberately simple: **create/prepare a draft → publish when ready → modify published material or withdraw previously published material**. No mandatory multi-person editorial approval workflow is introduced. This is conceptual stewardship, not exact authorization, public visibility, or an editor design.

An ordinary Vratarthi may create authored material; its forms, content types, visibility, and participation mechanisms remain PH-006 / DT-017. A Curator may discover valuable authored material and consider curated inclusion without formal submission. There is **no dedicated Vratarthi Resource submission/review/approval/rejection/promotion workflow in current scope**. Authored content is not automatically a curated Resource (PD-015), and this grants neither private-content access nor permission to appropriate it.

No Blog/Post entity, social feed, notification, compulsory reading, universal public-access policy, or homepage component follows from broad prominence. Exact permissions, publishing/access rules, and foundational-standing authority remain DT-012/028 (PH-008). Discovery/prominence and authoring/reference interactions remain DT-027 (PH-009/010/011); authored-content relationships remain DT-017.

**Subsequent PH-006 resolution:** [PD-025](../06-community-and-content/authored-content.mdx) establishes generic authored content and its publication lifecycle; [PD-026–PD-030](../06-community-and-content/README.mdx) establish related community concepts, resolving DT-017/003 conceptually. Authorship/curation/standing remain distinct, with no automatic Resource promotion or private access. DT-028 retains exact curated-inclusion policy. Authored-content working drafts do not alter the Resource lifecycle or reference-history model.

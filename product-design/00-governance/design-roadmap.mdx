# Design roadmap

> **Map:** This roadmap names the design areas we know about, their scope, and where work stands. It is a planning aid for traceability, not a fixed waterfall or a commitment to a delivery order.

Areas may overlap or be revisited together as understanding develops. Keep each phase ID stable once created; update its status as work changes. Deferred questions are tracked separately in the [deferred-topic register](./deferred-topics.mdx).

| Phase ID | Design area | Purpose and scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PH-001` | North Star / product foundation | Establish Veervrat’s aim, identity, agency principles, and definition of developmental success. | completed |
| `PH-002` | Actors & Relationships | Identify relevant actors and the nature of their relationships at a conceptual level. | current |
| `PH-003` | Canonical ontology / domain model | Define the concepts and their relationships that later design work relies on. | planned |
| `PH-004` | Practice model / Journey lifecycle | Describe how the संस्कार is undertaken over time, including Journey structure and lifecycle. | planned |
| `PH-005` | Knowledge & resources model | Organize knowledge and resource types, relationships, and stewardship. | planned |
| `PH-006` | Community & content model | Define community participation and content concepts before feature-level behavior. | planned |
| `PH-007` | Longitudinal history / self-review / evidence model | Describe how personal history, reflection, and evidence are represented over time. | planned |
| `PH-008` | Permissions & privacy model | Establish privacy and access principles and feature-level permission decisions. | planned |
| `PH-009` | Experience architecture | Connect the product’s capabilities into a coherent experience. | planned |
| `PH-010` | Information architecture / navigation | Organize information, destinations, and navigation. | planned |
| `PH-011` | Complete user journeys / states / edge cases | Specify user journeys, states, transitions, and important edge cases. | planned |
| `PH-012` | Brand & design language | Define the product’s verbal and visual design language. | planned |
| `PH-013` | Design system | Define reusable interface foundations and components. | planned |
| `PH-014` | Motion & interaction language | Define interaction feedback, transitions, and motion behavior. | planned |
| `PH-015` | Screen design / prototypes | Design and connect representative screens and prototypes. | planned |
| `PH-016` | Implementation specification / design QA | Prepare implementation guidance and a design-quality review approach. | planned |

## Confirmed planning direction and scope refinements

**Complete holistic product design through PH-016, then incrementally evolve the existing Veervrat application into the redesigned product.** This is redesign-first, not reuse-first: the approved target determines what implementation should become. The existing application/repository supplies a starting point and continuity context, not a constraint on target design or a default commitment to an unrelated ground-up application.

Evaluate each existing area for **retention, refactoring, redesign, replacement, removal, or supplementation** against the target. Reuse must be justified; redesign does not require indiscriminate rewriting. Existing functionality, architecture, component foundations, authentication, database models/connectivity, infrastructure, stack, and deployment pipelines are **not approved for retention merely because they exist**. Substantial subsystem restructuring remains possible. Incremental evolution does not mean one simultaneous release.

PH-009's completeness review examines participant/context coverage and connections among practice, knowledge, community, history, and guidance. Illustrative considerations include signed-in/public and first/returning experiences, landing, authentication/onboarding/invitations, contextual walkthroughs, help, settings, and account management across Vratarthi, Vratamitra, Curator, Moderator, Admin, and Public Visitor contexts. These are **not a committed or exhaustive feature list**: identify/evaluate needs, then explicitly decide them. Detailed flows/states/exceptions and screen design retain later-phase ownership. No separate capability map, new phase, or parallel governance framework is introduced.

PH-016 plans a credible existing-to-target transition: architecture/feasibility, implementation sequencing/readiness, migration/compatibility, existing-user and historical-data continuity, validation, safe release/recovery, and design QA. DT-020 tracks the remaining plan, including divergent old/new semantics, possible data transformations or legacy-history preservation. PH-016 specifies the transition; actual implementation and migration execution follow afterward. No full implementation or production-data audit is authorized in this PH-004 change.

Earlier phases may consult constraints or perform narrowly scoped feasibility investigation when a material uncertainty affects a product decision. Such investigation is **not authorization for premature implementation**. These refinements do not start PH-009/016 work or alter the confirmed domain model.

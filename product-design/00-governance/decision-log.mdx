# Decision log

> **Map:** The machine-readable registry is the index of redesign decisions. Each decision has one canonical record and an immutable `PD-*` identifier; this page explains how to use that index. Six user-confirmed North Star and agency decisions are recorded in the [canonical North Star](../01-product-model/north-star.mdx); the PH-002 relationship decisions are recorded in [Actors & Relationships](../02-actors-and-relationships/README.mdx), and the current identity/role ontology in [PH-003](../03-ontology-and-domain-model/README.mdx).

## Registry

See [`decision-registry.json`](./decision-registry.json) for the current records and [`decision-registry.schema.json`](./decision-registry.schema.json) for the record shape and allowed statuses.

PD-001 through PD-006 are user-confirmed North Star and agency decisions; PD-006 is a scoped application of PD-004. PD-007 and PD-008 are the confirmed PH-002 Primary and Journey Vratamitra relationship lifecycles; PD-009 records multiplicity, coexistence, and capacity; PD-010 (historical) records the original actor inventory; PD-011 supersedes it with the confirmed Identity, Participation, and Role Ontology, preserving actor purposes. User confirmation is their authority; historical source evidence and the subsequent interpretations are distinguished in the [PH-003 reconciliation](../03-ontology-and-domain-model/reconciliation.mdx). Historical application documentation does not override redesign decisions.

### Registry status meanings

- `confirmed` — explicitly confirmed by the user for the redesign.
- `proposed` — a candidate outcome awaiting confirmation.
- `open` — unresolved, unknown, or awaiting investigation.
- `superseded` — retained history replaced by a newer decision; requires `superseded_by`.
- `source-derived` — an interpretation grounded in a cited source, without user confirmation.

When a status changes, update the registry entry and canonical record together, preserve the ID, and explain the transition. A change to a confirmed decision requires a new decision record and an explicit supersession link.

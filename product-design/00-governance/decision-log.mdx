# Decision log

> **Map:** The machine-readable registry is the index of redesign decisions. Each decision has one canonical record and an immutable `PD-*` identifier; this page explains how to use that index. Six user-confirmed North Star and agency decisions are recorded in the [canonical North Star](../01-product-model/north-star.mdx); the PH-002 relationship decisions are recorded in [Actors & Relationships](../02-actors-and-relationships/README.mdx).

## Registry

See [`decision-registry.json`](./decision-registry.json) for the current records and [`decision-registry.schema.json`](./decision-registry.schema.json) for the record shape and allowed statuses.

PD-001 through PD-006 are user-confirmed North Star and agency decisions; PD-006 is a scoped application of PD-004. PD-007 and PD-008 are the confirmed PH-002 Primary and Journey Vratamitra relationship lifecycles. No sources are listed because no repository source has been designated as authoritative for these decisions. Do not treat historical application documentation as automatically authoritative over confirmed redesign decisions.

## Status meanings

- `confirmed` — explicitly confirmed by the user for the redesign.
- `proposed` — a candidate outcome awaiting confirmation.
- `open` — unresolved, unknown, or awaiting investigation.
- `superseded` — retained history replaced by a newer decision; requires `superseded_by`.
- `source-derived` — an interpretation grounded in a cited source, without user confirmation.

When a status changes, update the registry entry and canonical record together, preserve the ID, and explain the transition. A change to a confirmed decision requires a new decision record and an explicit supersession link.

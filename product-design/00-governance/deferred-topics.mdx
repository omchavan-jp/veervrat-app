# Deferred topics

> **Map:** This page summarizes questions intentionally left for later work. The canonical machine-readable records, including status and target phase, are in [`deferred-topics.json`](./deferred-topics.json). These topics are not product decisions.

When a discussion defers a question to a later phase, create or update its `DT-*` record. At phase start, review open topics assigned there; at phase end, resolve, move, drop with rationale, or keep each visibly open with an updated reason. See the [design roadmap](./design-roadmap.mdx) and [working method](./working-method.mdx).

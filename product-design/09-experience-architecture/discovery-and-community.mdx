# Purposeful discovery and community

> **Map:** Deliberate browsing/search and Discover/Following support knowledge and community. Curator selection, published authored content and official/foundational standing must remain distinct. Design for purposeful exploration, not compulsive consumption.

## PD-042 — Purposeful knowledge and community discovery

### Deliberate and incidental discovery

Support **deliberate browsing and searching** across knowledge, Resources, Sections, catalogs and accessible community writing. Related knowledge, practice and people's perspectives should connect naturally around a subject without forcing a Journey or pretending that every source has the same authority.

Alongside deliberate exploration, support a **dynamic, global, curator-led discovery experience**: selected Resources, references to particular foundational Sections, featured authored contributions and relevant curated material. Selection, ordering, rotation and prominence logic remain later design. Featuring an accessible authored item does **not** automatically adopt it as an Official/Foundational Resource or transfer authorship (PD-021/022/025/038).

Public community-authored material should remain **discoverable without Curator selection**. Public availability does not imply prominent promotion. Ordinary viewpoints and curator/foundational standing must be distinguishable.

The complementary discovery intentions are **Discover** (valuable material beyond one's followed people, including global curation and public community writing) and **Following** (accessible contributions from people deliberately followed). These belong to a broader coherent discovery experience, without committing to separate screens, two tabs, one mixed feed, a particular algorithm or endless scrolling. Following is approved/recipient-accepted and access remains audience-bound under PD-027/028/036/038. Content appearing in Following need not produce an outbound notification.

### Why the experience matters

**Scrolling itself is not the enemy**: a person thoughtfully reading for half an hour may be learning meaningfully, while compulsively refreshing for novelty is undesirable. Do not optimize for time spent, reactions, follower/popularity counts, posting frequency, constant novelty or artificial urgency. Even a non-personalized chronological feed can encourage compulsive use; absence of personalized ranking is **not sufficient**. Designers and owners should actively encourage purposeful exploration, reflection, learning and meaningful action, rather than rely on users to resist harmful engagement incentives. Preserve PD-004/005/006/025/026. There is no commitment to a conventional engagement-oriented social feed.

### Publishing, dialogue and steward responsibilities

Make publishing, sharing and thoughtful dialogue accessible without pressuring anyone to contribute for activity's sake. Quiet readers, occasional authors and active discussants can all participate meaningfully; discussion may be as valuable as an original article. Ease of publishing should not incentivize shallow posting. Established authored-content, drafts, nested comments, simple appreciation, following, messaging and moderation remain PD-025/026/027/028/030/037/038/039. No separate Blog/Post/Daily Log entities, mandatory posting cadence or additional public engagement metrics are introduced.

Curator experiences must also support knowledge/Resource stewardship and maintenance of **all four developmental catalogs**, including semantic-change awareness, retirement, valid relationships/dependencies, historical references and potential impact on existing assessments or Journeys (PD-013/021/022/023/035/038). These may justify specialized workspaces. Precise edit/withdraw/validation/reference flows are PH-011; terminology PH-012, interface design PH-013–015, implementation PH-016.

The community experience should connect content, Profiles, discussion, requests, following, private conversation and protective actions coherently without producing disconnected role-combination modes. Their detailed states, flows and layouts are deferred.

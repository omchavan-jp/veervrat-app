# Personal experience, Profiles and assessment

> **Map:** A Vratarthi should return to purposeful action, reflection and guidance rather than app-compliance tracking. Profiles support understanding and connection without developmental scorecards. Assessments inform but do not direct judgment.

## PD-041 — Personal development without scorecards

### Profile and personal experience are distinct

A **Profile** helps others identify, understand and connect with a Vratarthi, and discover contributions consciously shared with an appropriate audience. Published authored material or shared Journeys may be accessible there subject to their established audiences (PD-025, PD-028, PD-036, PD-038). Profile information does not own Journeys, assessments or developmental history (PD-011, PD-015).

Do not make personal development into automatically published scores: Journey, assessment and undertaking counts should not become public achievement displays or popularity comparisons. The old app's many per-field toggles should not be reproduced. Protect sensitive material through clear product and audience boundaries, not by making the person individually hide default-exposed activity. Basic Profile public access remains PD-036.

The initial Profile needs enough information for identification, contribution discovery and connection, **without requiring a biography or elaborate personalization**. Free-form self-introductions and self-described interests were considered and are **outside the approved initial scope**, not merely optional fields awaiting layout. They may be reconsidered later only through an explicit product decision. Exact minimal field set, edits, privacy explanations and presentation remain later work, respecting PD-036 and PH-011. A person's own landing/personal experience is conceptually distinct from what others see on their Profile, but is **not** a new domain entity or prescribed dashboard.

### Coherent Journey and history

An active Journey experience should connect the person's developmental intention, undertakings, significant changes, reflections, history and relevant Vratamitra guidance. Returning should reconnect them with meaningful real-life action, not first ask for logs or compliance evidence. The history model of PD-016, PD-017, PD-018, PD-031, PD-032, PD-033, PD-034, PD-035 and privacy in PD-036 remain authoritative. There is no mandatory daily logging, streak enforcement, automatic developmental judgment or generic Journey comment system.

People should be able to revisit past completed or discontinued Journeys and their meaningful histories. An integrated **cross-Journey reflection** experience may later help someone understand long-term development, but is a **strong uncommitted future candidate**, not essential to initial scope. Keep the existing underlying history coherent without inventing an aggregate progress scorecard or a parallel journal. PH-010/011 will decide navigation, states, revisions, recovery and conclusion flows.

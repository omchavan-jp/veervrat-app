# Attention, notifications and reminders

> **Map:** People may have several responsibilities, but Veervrat should surface matters genuinely relevant to them without guilt, fabricated urgency or dependence on constant reminders.

## PD-043 — Attention in service of agency

### Contextual and cross-context awareness

A Vratarthi, Vratamitra, Curator, Moderator or Admin can encounter requests or matters needing attention. Surface them **in the relevant context** and support simple **cross-context awareness** so important responsibilities are not missed. This does **not** require a universal inbox, an obligations dashboard, duplicate views or a task-management system. Dedicated responsibility-specific views or grouping may be justified by actual complexity in PH-010/011.

Not every pending item is urgent or an obligation. An ordinary message, guide perspective request and moderation case have different significance. The application should not make someone feel they are falling behind merely because they have not opened it recently.

### Discovery is not notification

Something worth discovering does **not** automatically deserve an active notification. A person followed publishing an article can be visible through Following without push or email. Direct communication, explicit requests or materially important responsibility-specific changes may deserve attention. In-context visibility and outbound notification are distinct. Existing silent-decline, access and communication policy remains PD-027/037/038. Do not invent an event/channel/urgency matrix now.

### Voluntary reminders and returning

Reminders should help people act on **intentions they own**, be optional, be easy to set/change/silence/stop, and add minimal friction. Starting a Journey or undertaking does not require reminder setup; an undertaking's desired practice frequency does **not** automatically become a notification cadence. Do not build an elaborate reminder-management workflow merely because reminders are supported.

Avoid unsolicited inactivity nudges, recording-compliance prompts, streak anxiety and guilt-based re-engagement. A person can practise meaningfully in real life without opening the app. On return, help them reconnect with their intentions and genuinely relevant matters, not an artificial backlog. These requirements extend PD-004/006 and do not change observed engagement/lifecycle semantics from PD-016.

Notification-producing events, recipients, channels, timing, scheduling, deduplication, statuses, preferences, routing and detailed user interactions remain in DT-008 for PH-011/013–016, not this experience-level decision.

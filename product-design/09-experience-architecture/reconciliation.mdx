## Participant and context completeness

- **Public/unsigned:** useful open knowledge, public authored contributions and basic Profiles without compulsory signup.
- **New/returning registered Vratarthi:** practice may be active, concluded or not started; exploration and community still provide value; no performance-pressure dashboard.
- **Article visitor becoming registered:** original intention and eligible action preserved through authentication/registration; no unrelated forced tour.
- **Invitation recipient:** context retained; registration does not accept guidance.
- **Vratamitra guiding several Vratarthis:** self remains a Vratarthi, guidance contextual, no separate identity.
- **Curator, Moderator, Admin, including overlapping responsibilities:** specialized work may have dedicated experiences, not every role permutation or a single cluttered dashboard.
- **Help, safety and account needs:** relevant instructions, audience clarity, reporting/blocking/appeals, settings and account management accessible when needed.

These are experience-architecture coverage checks, **not detailed PH-011 user journeys**.

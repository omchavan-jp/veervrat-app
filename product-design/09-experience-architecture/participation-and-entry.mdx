# Participation, entry and supporting experiences

> **Map:** Every registered person is a Vratarthi, whose participation can involve practice, learning and community. Guidance and granted capabilities add responsibilities without replacing that identity. Entry, return, help and account tasks should not break the person's intention.

## PD-040 — Coherent participation and continuity

### One Vratarthi with additional responsibilities

Every registered participant is a Vratarthi (PD-011), including when they have no active Journey. Exploration, learning, participation in community and reflection are meaningful parts of their Veervrat experience; a Journey should not be imposed as an entry gate or the sole reason to use the platform.

A person may guide several Vratarthis while undertaking their own Journey(s), and simultaneously hold Curator, Moderator or Admin capabilities. These are **additive contexts, not alternative account identities**. A responsibility-specific workspace or distinct experience may be justified, but do **not** produce a special interface for every combination such as Vratamitra-plus-Curator. Coherence does not require forcing all work into one dashboard; it requires a natural relationship among the experiences. Navigation and workspace details remain PH-010. Permissions and role boundaries remain PD-007/008/009/011/036/038/039.

### First encounter, understanding and intent preservation

Public Visitors can read public knowledge, published material and basic Profiles unsigned under PD-036. A visitor should be helped to recognize the personal relevance of Veervrat, understand what undertaking Veervrat involves, and explore before deciding whether to register or start a Journey. The app should not tell them what they ought to develop.

**Carry the person's purpose forward** across public exploration, login, registration, onboarding and invitation contexts. Someone opening an article, initiating an allowed action or arriving by invitation should be able to resume that intention afterward when eligible, not be diverted to an unrelated landing. Preserve the intended action, not just a URL. Registration must never silently accept a Vratamitra relationship (PD-011); explicit choices and permission checks still apply. Safe redirects, expiry, retries, provider details and invitation routing remain DT-005/011 in PH-011.

Understanding Veervrat's philosophy, understanding application capabilities, and getting contextual walkthrough/help are different needs. Explanation should be available when useful, not automatically presented as one long mandatory tour. The exact types, number, sequence, timing and visual design of walkthroughs are **not PH-009 decisions**. Relevant organization and flows belong to PH-010/011; interaction and screen design to PH-013–015.

### Supporting experiences

Help should be accessible in context without requiring tutorials for ordinary actions. Settings, preferences, privacy and account management should be understandable with minimal configuration burden. An item's audience should be understandable at the point of sharing, while general preferences need predictable access. Reporting, blocking, appeals and other safety mechanisms should be accessible when needed without dominating normal participation. These are experience principles applying to already-confirmed capabilities (PD-028/030/036/037/038/039), not new screen or permission decisions.

### Boundaries

No homepage, default dashboard, role switcher, personalization system, onboarding gate or technical return-to mechanism is selected here. Past application behavior does not become a new requirement merely because it exists.

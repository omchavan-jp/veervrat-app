# Product model

> **Map:** This area records the concepts and decisions that define what Veervrat is for. Begin with the [confirmed North Star](./north-star.mdx). Its five decisions establish the aim, the relationship between गुणसंवर्धन and दोषापनयन, the distinction between Veervrat, its process, and its app, the primacy of Vratarthi agency, and the meaning of developmental success.

## Current scope

The North Star is confirmed. This page does not define the later practice model, journey structure, or screen behavior. Those areas remain for subsequent discussion and must stay traceable to confirmed decisions.

# Veervrat North Star

> **Map:** Veervrat is a संस्कार undertaken so that कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन increases. Development happens through cultivating relevant गुण / उपगुण, with the corresponding उणीव gradually weakening. The structured process and app support the संस्कार while the Vratarthi retains agency. Success means developmental change in life; measurements are evidence, not objectives. The six foundation decisions remain intact; PD-012 adds the confirmed philosophical refinement.

## PD-001 — Veervrat North Star

Veervrat aims at progressively increasing **कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन**.

The intended human development is that the motivation, judgment, virtues, capacity for action, perseverance, adaptability, and self-reflection required for कर्तृत्व increasingly arise from within the person. This enables the person to independently recognize and choose meaningful aims, mobilize themselves and others where appropriate, navigate difficulty, and undertake increasingly meaningful and consequential action.

Self-reliance does not mean doing everything alone. The locus of agency is internal. Mentorship, help, collaboration, tools, institutions, and delegation are compatible with self-reliance when the Vratarthi deliberately recognizes and uses that support.

कर्तृत्वशक्ती does not increase only through external success. An intelligent, courageous, reflective attempt may represent substantial growth even when external circumstances cause it to fail. Societal contribution is an important dimension of meaningful action, but not the entirety of the concept.

## PD-002 — दोषापनयन through गुणसंवर्धन

Lacunae help a Vratarthi identify constraints on their कर्तृत्व; Veervrat should not encourage the person to identify with deficiency. Development is fundamentally framed through **गुणसंवर्धन**: cultivating the relevant गुण / उपगुण so that the corresponding lacuna gradually weakens.

Identifying an **उणीव is optional**: a Vratarthi may begin a Journey directly from a **गुणदर्शक विधान** (PD-014). उणीवा can provide developmental direction; **दोषापनयन** is the weakening of the corresponding उणीव through **गुणसंवर्धन**, not the discovery step itself.

When an उणीव is identified, the conceptual progression is:

**Lacuna identified → understand what it obstructs → identify relevant गुण / उपगुण → cultivate them through practice → lacuna weakens → कर्तृत्वशक्ती increases**

The existing conceptual work mapping lacunae to उपगुण is part of this structured knowledge.

## PD-003 — Veervrat, process, and app are distinct

**Veervrat is a संस्कार undertaken so that कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन increases.** A compact formulation is:

> कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन वाढावे यासाठी केलेला संस्कार म्हणजे वीरव्रत.

A structured process, workflow, or method helps the Vratarthi undertake and live this संस्कार. It includes understanding, identifying lacunae, assessment, choosing a Sentence, undertaking a Journey, Exposures, Resolutions, Challenge(s), reflection, Vratamitra guidance, and continued development.

The **Veervrat app** is the digital platform through which the Veervrat community engages with the संस्कार. It supports individual practice and documentation; organized access to knowledge and resources; Vratamitra guidance; interaction among people in the Veervrat community; and preservation of longitudinal personal history and evidence across practice.

Veervrat itself must not be reduced to either the workflow or the software. **Do not call Veervrat itself a framework or practice.**

### Scoped terminology rule: ERC

ERC may be used as internal shorthand in design and development discussions, but it is not Vratarthi-facing product language. Product copy uses the actual concepts: **Exposures, Resolutions, Challenges**. This is a scoped terminology rule recorded with PD-003; it is not a separate decision ID.

## PD-004 — Agency is strengthened, not substituted

Veervrat progressively strengthens the Vratarthi's agency rather than replacing it. The locus of judgment and action remains with the Vratarthi.

- The structured process provides structure, concepts, practices, and accumulated knowledge without eliminating personal judgment.
- A Vratamitra acts as a catalyst: questioning, supporting, challenging, offering perspective, and helping create conditions for development. The Vratamitra does not own the Vratarthi's Veervrat or become the permanent source of motivation or action.
- The app organizes, remembers, connects, recommends, and reduces friction. Continued dependence on the app is not the objective.
- AI may help interpret information, surface patterns, suggest possibilities, organize reflection, and explain reasoning. It remains assistive and contestable; it does not define who the Vratarthi is or decide their development.
- The community can provide experiences, perspectives, examples, resources, and interaction without turning Veervrat into conformity, popularity competition, or social-pressure-driven development.

### PD-006 — Notifications support self-directed action

This principle narrows how the agency commitment in **PD-004** applies to notifications and re-engagement. Notifications may inform, remind, and surface commitments, requests, responses, or relevant changes. They may help the Vratarthi remember and act on intentions the Vratarthi already owns.

Notifications and re-engagement must not become the primary external force that continually makes the Vratarthi act. Do not optimize them for compulsive engagement, streak anxiety, artificial urgency, or dependence on repeated prompting. This is a constraint on product behavior, not a ban on reminders or re-engagement. The notification model and its mechanics remain deferred to later design phases.

## PD-005 — Success is developmental change

At the Journey level, success means that the quality or capability expressed by the chosen Sentence becomes more genuinely, consistently, and independently present in the Vratarthi's life.

Evidence may include a meaningful shift in the Vratarthi's own assessment of the Sentence (for example, Sometimes → Often); completing and genuinely engaging with relevant Exposures and Resolutions; applying that practice through relevant Challenge(s); concrete experiences, choices, behaviours, and decisions in real life; reflection and evidence recorded during the Journey; and observations or validation from a Vratamitra where one is involved.

None of those signals alone defines success. Ratings, completion states, and other app metrics are evidence or proxies for underlying developmental change, not objectives to optimize in themselves.

At the broader Veervrat level, success is the progressive internalization and application of the qualities required for कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन.

**Challenge cardinality remains open.** This decision does not establish whether a Journey has exactly one Challenge or multiple Challenges; use “Challenge(s)” or equivalent neutral wording until the later practice-model discussion.

**Subsequent resolution:** The paragraph above preserves the scope left open when PD-005 was confirmed. PD-014 now resolves cardinality under DT-001; use its current ontology. PD-005's developmental-success principle is unchanged.

## PD-012 — व्रतपालन and समाजाभिमुखता within the North Star

This is an explicit additive refinement of PD-001, PD-002, and PD-003, not a replacement North Star. Their approved records remain intact. Veervrat remains a **संस्कार**, distinct from the application. **व्रतपालन / संकल्पपालन is fundamental and constitutive of that संस्कार**, not simply another app activity.

**गुणसंवर्धन is central**; उणीवा can help identify developmental direction, while दोषापनयन occurs through cultivating the relevant गुण / उपगुण. **क्षुद्र हृदयदौर्बल्यावर मात करणे** is understood as something overcome in pursuit of **कर्तृत्वशक्तीचे स्वावलंबन**, not a separate North Star.

**समाजाभिमुखता is an essential orientation:** developing कर्तृत्वशक्ती extends into meaningful participation in society rather than remaining purely inward-looking. This does not require compulsory social service or a socially beneficial outcome from every Journey. External observability of an achievement alone is not समाजाभिमुखता.

These are user-confirmed philosophical interpretations from the PH-003 discussions, not quotations attributed to historical literature. The [reconciliation and provenance record](../03-ontology-and-domain-model/reconciliation.mdx) distinguishes the available source evidence from these interpretations.

The [Journey ontology](../03-ontology-and-domain-model/journey-and-undertakings.mdx), PD-014, now resolves the earlier open cardinality question: अनुभव and आव्हान are optional, multiple undertakings are supported, and a completed Journey conceptually requires at least one completed संकल्प. PD-005's developmental-success principle remains unchanged; detailed lifecycle mechanics stay deferred.

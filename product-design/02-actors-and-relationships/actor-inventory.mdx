# Actor inventory

> **Map:** Public Visitors use public areas without signing in. Every signed-in user is a Vratarthi. Vratamitra, Curator, Moderator, and Admin are additional roles/capabilities a Vratarthi may hold; Primary and Journey Vratamitra describe guidance relationships. Identity/account ontology is carried forward to PH-003.

## PD-010 — Actor inventory and role structure

| Actor | Confirmed purpose and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Public Visitor | A person using publicly accessible parts of Veervrat without being signed in. They are not yet a Vratarthi in the product. |
| Vratarthi | Any signed-in user of Veervrat. Signing in makes the person a Vratarthi in the product model. |
| Vratamitra | A Vratarthi supporting another Vratarthi's development through a guidance relationship. An additional role/capability, not a mutually exclusive identity. |
| Primary Vratamitra | A Vratamitra in the continuing, broad, longitudinal guidance relationship defined by PD-007. |
| Journey Vratamitra | A Vratamitra involved in Journey-specific guidance as defined by PD-008. |
| Curator | A Vratarthi with stewardship responsibility for canonical Veervrat knowledge/content and centrally curated resources. |
| Moderator | A Vratarthi responsible for moderating user-generated/community content and its appropriateness for different visibility contexts on the platform. Exact scopes, visibility rules, actions, and permissions remain deferred. |
| Admin | A Vratarthi with broader operational/product-administration responsibilities required to operate Veervrat. Exact authority and permissions remain deferred. |

These are not mutually exclusive person identities. **Vratarthi is the base signed-in participation role.** Vratamitra, Curator, Moderator, and Admin are additional roles/capabilities a Vratarthi may hold. Primary and Journey Vratamitra describe particular guidance relationships. Multiplicity and coexistence are defined by PD-009.

## Carry-forward

PH-003 must define the canonical identity/account ontology: the relationship between person, signed-in user/account, Vratarthi, profile, roles/capabilities, and relationship assignments, preserving the confirmed rule that every signed-in user is a Vratarthi. This is tracked by DT-010; no account or database entity structure is established here.

Moderator mechanics and visibility/scoping are tracked by DT-003, with permission reconciliation in PH-008. Curator/resource mechanics remain under DT-004. Admin authority and permissions remain under DT-012 / PH-008.

# Vratamitra multiplicity, coexistence, and capacity

> **Map:** A person may guide multiple Vratarthis and Journeys while remaining a Vratarthi in their own Veervrat process. Primary and explicitly chosen specific-person Journey relationships may coexist for the same pair. There is no separate eligibility gate or arbitrary numerical guidance cap at this stage; capacity rests with human judgment. A Vratarthi cannot be their own Vratamitra.

## PD-009 — Vratamitra multiplicity, coexistence, and capacity

### Multiplicity and coexistence

- One Vratarthi may serve as Primary Vratamitra for multiple other Vratarthis.
- One person may serve as specific-person Journey Vratamitra for multiple Journeys and/or multiple Vratarthis.
- Serving as someone's Vratamitra does not stop a person from simultaneously being a Vratarthi in their own Veervrat process.
- For the same Vratarthi–Vratamitra pair, a person may simultaneously be the Vratarthi's Primary Vratamitra and hold one or more specific-person Journey Vratamitra relationships where explicitly chosen.

The corresponding relationship lifecycles remain canonical in [PD-007](./primary-vratamitra-relationship.mdx) and [PD-008](./journey-vratamitra-relationship.mdx).

### Eligibility and self-relationship

- Any Vratarthi may serve as a Vratamitra. There is no separate eligibility, qualification, certification, or gate at this stage.
- A Vratarthi cannot be their own Vratamitra.

### Capacity

- Veervrat imposes no arbitrary hard numerical cap on how many Vratarthis a person may guide as Primary Vratamitra or how many Journey Vratamitra relationships they may hold.
- Capacity is currently left to human judgment rather than enforced through a product limit.
- This does not preclude later UX that helps a Vratamitra understand their current guidance load. No workload UI, notifications, permissions, scheduling, capacity indicators, or implementation are decided here.

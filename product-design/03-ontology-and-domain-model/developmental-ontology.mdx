# Developmental concepts and self-assessment

> **Map:** गुण, उपगुण, उणीवा, and गुणदर्शक विधाने are shared curated catalogs. उणीवा guide discovery through उपगुण; a विधान expresses a quality in life. स्वमूल्यांकन records point-in-time self-understanding, independently of undertaking a Journey.

## PD-013 — Developmental catalogs and स्वमूल्यांकन

| Concept / relationship | Confirmed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| गुण → उपगुण | Each उपगुण belongs to exactly one गुण. |
| उणीव ↔ उपगुण | Many-to-many. उणीवा help discover developmental direction; they are not the objective of व्रतपालन. |
| उपगुण → गुणदर्शक विधान | Each विधान belongs to exactly one उपगुण. |
| उणीव ↔ विधान | No direct canonical association. Discovery traverses the associated उपगुण. |

Use **उणीव**, plural **उणीवा**. **गुणदर्शक विधान / Virtue Expression** is the provisional name for the agreed concept previously called a Sentence in PD-005 and PD-011's surrounding product vocabulary. Naming remains provisional; the meaning is confirmed:

> एखादा उपगुण व्यक्तीच्या विचारांत, वृत्तीत, क्षमतेत, निर्णयांत किंवा कृतींत कसा प्रकट होतो, हे व्यक्त करणारे सकारात्मक विधान.

> A positive statement expressing how a particular sub-virtue manifests in a person's everyday life—through their thoughts, attitudes, abilities, decisions, or actions.

All four catalogs are shared and curated in the current design. Personal उणीवा and personal गुणदर्शक विधाने are uncommitted future candidates only; neither current nor future feature support is approved.

### स्वमूल्यांकन

Assessment captures the Vratarthi's self-understanding at a particular point in time. It is distinct from व्रतपालन and from proof of success, and may exist independently of a Journey (consistent with PD-005).

- Responses evaluate individual गुणदर्शक विधाने, not गुण, उपगुण, or उणीवा directly.
- Current entry is उणीव-based. The assessed statements are derived through that उणीव's associated उपगुण; for now, **all statements** belonging to those उपगुण are included.
- One assessment instance contains multiple responses; assessments may be repeated over time.
- This introduces neither an independent catalog of assessment definitions nor direct उणीव–statement mappings.

Rating scales, scoring, presentation, and interaction mechanics remain DT-016. Provisional naming remains DT-019. Catalog/resource stewardship mechanics remain DT-004. This is a conceptual ontology, not a persistence design.

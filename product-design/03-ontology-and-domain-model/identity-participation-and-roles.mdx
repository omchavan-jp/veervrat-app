# Identity, Participation, and Role Ontology

> **Map:** Each registered Veervrat account represents exactly one persistent Vratarthi identity and automatically has a Profile. Authentication methods establish access to that same account. Curator, Moderator, and Admin are granted capabilities; Vratamitra roles arise through guidance relationships. Registration through an invitation does not accept the guidance relationship.

## PD-011 — Identity, Participation, and Role Ontology

### Registration, identity, and authentication

Every registered Veervrat account represents exactly one Vratarthi. **Vratarthi status begins with registration and persists across login/logout.** Authentication determines whether someone is currently signed in; it neither creates nor removes that status. A registered Vratarthi browsing while logged out has Public Visitor access without losing their underlying Vratarthi identity.

Registration, account identity, participation identity, and authenticated session state are distinct concepts. One account may have multiple authentication methods, all authenticating the same persistent account/Vratarthi identity. Different methods do not inherently create different Vratarthis.

One real-world human may create multiple accounts; each represents an independent Vratarthi. Veervrat does not establish or enforce unique real-world identity per account, impose one account per human, or automatically merge accounts that may belong to the same human. A separate canonical Person entity is not currently needed. The self-Vratamitra prohibition in PD-009 applies at the account/identity level. No identity-verification, merging, or abuse-prevention mechanics follow from this rule.

### Profile

Every registered Vratarthi automatically has a Profile, including when optional personal details have not been supplied. Profile completion is not required for participation. Account identity and Profile are conceptually distinct; this does not require separate database tables. Actual fields, public visibility, editing permissions, and Profile UI remain deferred under DT-014.

### Participation, capabilities, and contextual roles

| Category | Confirmed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Base participation identity | Vratarthi is the base participation identity of every registered Veervrat account. Public Visitor describes unsigned-in public access, including access by an already-registered Vratarthi. |
| Explicitly granted platform capabilities | Curator, Moderator, and Admin are additional capabilities granted to Vratarthis. They may coexist with Vratarthi participation and with one another. |
| Contextual, relationship-derived roles | Roles arise from relationships involving people, content, or activities; they do not inherently require separate platform-role assignments. |

The PH-002 actor purposes are preserved:

- **Vratamitra:** a Vratarthi supporting another Vratarthi's development through guidance. No separately granted platform-role membership is required; any Vratarthi may become another's Vratamitra through the relevant relationship.
- **Primary Vratamitra:** continuing, broad, longitudinal guidance through the accepted Primary relationship in PD-007.
- **Journey Vratamitra:** Journey-specific guidance under PD-008. Specific-person guidance arises through the accepted Journey relationship; Primary-derived guidance continues to use the accepted Primary relationship without separate Journey acceptance.
- **Curator:** stewardship of canonical Veervrat knowledge/content and centrally curated resources.
- **Moderator:** moderation of user-generated/community content and its appropriateness for different platform visibility contexts; exact scopes, visibility rules, actions, and permissions remain deferred.
- **Admin:** broader operational/product-administration responsibility required to operate Veervrat; exact authority and permissions remain deferred.

PD-007, PD-008, and PD-009 retain their eligibility, cardinality, lifecycle, multiplicity, and coexistence rules. A contextual relationship does not automatically grant unrestricted authority or permissions; those remain separate concerns under DT-002 and related permission records.

**Author** (relationship to content authored) and **Commenter** (relationship to a comment/activity) are illustrative contextual-role examples only. They do not establish blogs, posts, comments, or any specific content feature. Content models remain for PH-005/PH-006.

### Invitations and registration

A person may be invited to join Veervrat. A Vratarthi may also invite someone to become their Primary or specific-person Journey Vratamitra. The intended recipient may already be registered or may not yet have an account, so a Vratamitra invitation may exist before recipient registration. This does not require a separate canonical Person entity.

**Registration through a Vratamitra invitation does not establish the guidance relationship.** The recipient must explicitly accept that relationship invitation, consistently with PD-007 and PD-008. This requirement does not add separate Journey acceptance to Primary-derived guidance.

### Supersession and deferred boundaries

PD-011 supersedes [PD-010 (historical)](../02-actors-and-relationships/actor-inventory.mdx). It preserves the intention that every signed-in user is a Vratarthi and the established actor purposes/coexistence, while correcting the earlier literal implication that signing in creates participation identity or logging out removes it. Registration establishes the persistent identity. It also distinguishes granted capabilities from relationship-derived roles.

DT-010 is resolved by this conceptual cluster. Remaining work is traceable separately: DT-005 covers authentication methods/providers, account-linking and return-to-context flows; DT-011 covers invitation delivery/matching/routing and UX; DT-009 retains invitation expiry; DT-008 retains notifications. DT-012 covers granting authority, scope, revocation, and operational mechanics for granted capabilities; DT-003/004 retain moderation and curation mechanics. DT-013 covers account deactivation/deletion, identity termination, retention, relationship/contribution implications, and account-management UX (PH-008, reconciled in PH-011).

This cluster does not establish account-exit behavior, implementation data models, providers, token/session architecture, APIs, technical IDs, detailed permissions, or screens. Conceptually distinct does not mean separately persisted.

# Journey and undertakings

> **Map:** A Journey is a Vratarthi's personal undertaking of one shared गुणदर्शक विधान. Discovery contexts belong to that Journey's history. अनुभव, संकल्प, and आव्हान distinguish kinds of undertaking; undertaking catalog items (earlier “shared suggestions”) and personal undertakings are distinct.

## PD-014 — Journey and अनुभव / संकल्प / आव्हान ontology

### Journey anchor and discovery context

A Journey belongs to the Vratarthi and undertakes **exactly one गुणदर्शक विधान**, which exists independently in the shared catalog (PD-013). A Journey may begin without selecting an उणीव or performing स्वमूल्यांकन.

A Journey may preserve zero, one, or multiple उणीवा as historical discovery/selection contexts. These are Journey-specific associations, not universal उणीव–विधान mappings. Encountering the same विधान through another उणीव during an ongoing Journey must not automatically create a duplicate Journey; preserve the additional discovery context. Exact choice/automatic reuse behavior remains DT-015 in PH-004.

After completing a Journey, the same Vratarthi may begin a distinct new Journey for the same विधान. PD-007, PD-008, and PD-009 retain their guidance semantics; this record does not add permissions or guidance lifecycle states.

### Kinds of undertaking

| Concept | Confirmed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अनुभव — Exposure | An experience deliberately undertaken to explore or develop the chosen quality, typically once or a few times. May involve observation, participation, interaction, experimentation, or other experiences. Challenge, unfamiliarity, and success are not mandatory characteristics. |
| संकल्प — Resolution | A sustained or recurring commitment to cultivate the chosen quality. संकल्पपालन is central to Veervrat; a tangible final product is not required. |
| आव्हान — Challenge | A sufficiently demanding undertaking applying developing qualities to achieve a significant, tangible result recognizable by at least one other person. It need not be public or have direct social impact, and is not merely a quantitatively larger ordinary संकल्प. |

These are undertaken experiences/commitments/challenges, not an approval of experience posts or a generic logging feature. ERC remains internal shorthand only under PD-003's terminology rule.

### Shared suggestions and personal undertakings

**PH-005 terminology clarification:** [PD-023](../05-knowledge-and-resources/undertaking-catalogs.mdx) names the reusable shared/catalog suggestions below **undertaking catalog items**. This preserves PD-014's original wording and meaning; presenting relevant entries is not an undertaking recommendation mechanism. Custom Vratamitra suggestions are still meaningful.

All three support reusable suggestions in shared catalogs, with **many-to-many** associations between catalog suggestions and गुणदर्शक विधाने. A Journey supports multiple Journey-specific undertakings. Custom Journey-specific suggestions may come from the Vratarthi or Journey Vratamitra; **final authority remains with the Vratarthi**, consistent with PD-004. Detailed contribution permissions remain DT-002.

Custom suggestions are not automatically promoted to shared catalogs. Whether/how promotion occurs remains DT-004 in PH-005. **Subsequent resolution:** PD-023 establishes Curator stewardship with no dedicated Vratarthi submission/promotion workflow in current scope; DT-004 is resolved conceptually, with permissions and experience residuals recorded in the [PH-005 reconciliation](../05-knowledge-and-resources/reconciliation.mdx).

Conceptually, a completed Journey requires **at least one completed संकल्प**; अनुभव and आव्हान are optional. Multiple आव्हाने are supported; DT-001's one-versus-many question is resolved here. These requirements do not equate completion with developmental success (PD-005), nor make tangible external observability equivalent to समाजाभिमुखता (PD-012).

Detailed completion, iteration, recurrence, exceptions, and participation workflows remain PH-004: DT-007 covers आव्हान workflows and DT-015 covers the Journey/अनुभव/संकल्प mechanics, explicitly including **संकल्प reiteration/refinement**. Broader aggregate progression or readiness to formally undertake Veervrat remains DT-018; no numerical milestone is approved.

# Resources, Profile, and authored-content boundaries

> **Map:** A Resource supports learning or development. Profile information is distinct from developmental records, which belong to the Vratarthi. Authored content does not automatically become a curated Resource. Detailed structure, content features, and access remain deferred.

## PD-015 — Resource and personal-information boundaries

### Resource

A **Resource** is material made accessible through Veervrat to support understanding, learning, or personal development. It may be created/published by Veervrat or sourced externally. **Handbook and Pothi are both foundational documents and accessible Resources.**

Books, articles, videos, lectures, and other formats need not become distinct domain entity types solely because their formats differ. Resources need not be associated with गुण, उपगुण, or गुणदर्शक विधाने.

DT-004 in PH-005 owns classification/internal structure; Pothi, श्लोक, and section-level modeling; potential developmental-concept relationships; creation, publishing, curation, promotion, and custom-suggestion promotion mechanisms. Publishing permissions reconcile in PH-008. This decision introduces no separate Pothi, श्लोक, Blog, or Video entities.

### Profile and developmental records

Preserve PD-011's Account/Vratarthi/Profile distinction. **Profile information and developmental records are conceptually separate. Journeys and assessments belong to the Vratarthi, not the Profile.** A future Profile page may display information from these records without owning them conceptually. No Profile fields, visibility, editing permissions, or page behavior are established.

DT-014 carries the remaining Profile fields/presentation into PH-009, with PH-008 permission and PH-011 journey reconciliation. Its PH-003 conceptual boundary is addressed here; its original deferral history is preserved.

### Authored content

User-authored content is **not automatically a curated Resource**. Whether Posts, Blogs, feeds, or other community mechanisms exist, and their content/participation concepts, remain DT-017 in PH-006. Whether/how authored material can enter curated Resources reconciles with DT-004. Relevant access and publishing permissions remain PH-008 (DT-012 and the feature-level work).

Historical Daily Logs or experience posts do not establish redesign features. This cluster adds no standalone Reflection, Evidence, Daily Log, Post, or other unconfirmed domain concept. Earlier approved references to reflection/evidence remain intact without defining their ontology here.

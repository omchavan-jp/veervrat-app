# Marathi policy review — terms of use and privacy policy

**For a native Marathi speaker.** Prepared 2026-08-25. Nachiket Nitsure is the natural reviewer,
having reviewed the उणीव terminology sweep.

---

## What this is, and why it is not ordinary copy review

Veervrat publishes its **terms of use** and **privacy policy** in English and Marathi. Someone
accepting the Marathi version is agreeing to *that text* — so a mistranslation is not a cosmetic
problem. It is a person consenting to something other than what was meant.

**The English is authoritative.** Where the two differ, the English says what we actually do; the
Marathi is a first translation and has never been reviewed.

---

## What we are asking

Read each pair below and say, for each, whether the Marathi means the same thing as the English.
Not whether it is elegant — whether it is **accurate**, and whether an ordinary reader would take
the same meaning from it.

Three passages matter more than the rest:

1. **What deletion does.** The document describes what is removed and what is kept when someone
   deletes their account. Overstating removal would be a promise we do not keep.
2. **A vratmitra's private notes.** A mentor records observations about the person they guide.
   This is the most sensitive disclosure in the document, and the one most easily softened by a
   translation that reads more gently than the English.
3. **The 18+ statement.** The platform is adults-only, and the Marathi has to say so as plainly
   as the English does.

Also worth flagging, wherever you see it:

- an English term left untranslated where a natural Marathi word exists
- a translation that is *more reassuring* than the English — this is the failure mode that
  matters, because it produces consent to something better than what we do
- anything that reads as machine-translated rather than written

## How to give it back

Marking the block number and what is wrong is enough — a corrected sentence is welcome but not
expected. There is no need to work in the software.

⚠️ **If a correction changes meaning materially, we will bump the document version**, which
re-prompts everyone who accepted the earlier text to accept the new one. That is the mechanism
working as intended, not an inconvenience — so please do not soften a real correction to avoid
triggering it.
